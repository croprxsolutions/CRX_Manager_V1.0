## 2026-11-19 - An inactive customer gets no new orders, deliveries, holds or spray jobs (#2000)

- Migration `20270129200000_inactive_customer_refuses_new_work.sql` carries out Mason's 2026-11-19 decision: an inactive customer must be made active again before new work is done for them. The database refuses a new order, delivery, stock hold or spray job for an inactive customer, and refuses reopening closed work (undeleting, switching a hold back on, pushing out an expired hold) or moving any order, delivery, hold or job onto one, open or closed. A delivery is checked against its own customer and its order's customer. Holds: every hold not built from other work is checked; a hold built from a job, quote or booking follows its source, so starting an existing job still works. It applies to every role and every path; the check runs after the normal permission check, so someone without permission learns nothing about the customer.
- Existing work of an inactive customer can still be edited, part-filled, cancelled, deleted or released. The one live inactive customer with 2 confirmed orders stays as it is; delivering them needs the customer reactivated first.
- Closes KI-148's accepted timing gap: the check locks the customer row, so a deactivation and a new piece of work saved at the same moment can no longer both go through.
- Not here (Taha's quote, invoice and booking work until 2026-12-15): quotes, invoices and bookings for inactive customers.
- Screen: the refusal sentence is shown as written (`errorSanitizer`); the New Delivery screen now uses it too.
