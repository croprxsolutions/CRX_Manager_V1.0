## 2026-11-05 — Transfer-created invoices follow the customer's payment terms (KI-14)

Migration `20261112090000_transfer_invoice_due_date_follows_terms.sql` stops "Transfer to invoice" from stamping every new field invoice as due 30 days after the invoice date. The transfer now stamps the due date from the billed customer's terms with `parse_payment_terms_days`, the helper the posting path already uses, so it is the date posting itself would set: Net 15 is due in 15 days, Net 30 or blank terms in 30, due on receipt the same day. It also copies those terms onto the invoice (a customer with blank terms gets `Net 30`, the 30 days the date already uses), so the PDF's printed terms and its due date come from the same moment even if the customer's terms change before posting, and the invoice screens recognise the date as the terms' own rather than a custom date. Posting keeps both. The date is stamped at transfer rather than left blank because an unposted invoice can be emailed, and the emailed PDF prints a blank due date as "On receipt" (both found by the Sol review). Saving terms on either invoice screen still clears the due date, so posting re-derives it from the new terms. Both the single-invoice and the multi-owner split paths change; nothing else in the transfer body changes.

The field-application screen's billing save writes one set of terms to every invoice in a split. Because a split's owners can now carry different terms, that save now refuses (`SPLIT_INVOICE_TERMS_DIFFER`) when they differ, instead of silently moving every owner to one owner's terms. Single invoices, and splits whose owners share terms, save as before. On 2026-11-05 every live customer was Net 30 or blank, so no split can hold different terms yet.

Invoices already saved keep their due dates. On 2026-11-05 live had one transfer-created invoice, and its due date already matched its customer's terms (every live customer is Net 30 or blank), so no saved invoice disagrees with its terms.

Not changed here: the field-application screen's terms picker shows Net 30 when an invoice has no terms of its own, and saving writes that onto the invoice. Transfer invoices now carry their customer's terms, so this no longer reaches them, but it still applies to other field invoices with blank terms. Also not changed: the shared terms reader takes the first number in the terms, so `2/10 Net 30` would mean 2 days; posting already reads every invoice that way, and no live customer has such terms. Both are tracked under KI-14, with editing different terms on a split from that screen.

Proven on a disposable real-schema database by `scripts/smoke/prove-transfer-invoice-due-date-real-schema.mjs` (transfer, then post, for Net 15, Net 30, due on receipt and a split, plus a blank-terms customer, a customer whose terms change between transfer and posting, and the split save refusal). This candidate has not been applied to production.
