The sales tax checkbox on a chemical sale: one rate from Settings (6.25), on its own "Sales tax 6.25%" line after the standing discount, per payer, to the cent.

**What it does.**
- `app_settings` row `sales_tax_rate` = `6.25` (admin edits it, as every setting). A CHECK accepts only a plain percentage (0 to 99.999, at most three decimals), so a typo can't stop drafting. A missing row refuses a taxed draft (`SALES_TAX_RATE_MISSING`) instead of taxing at nothing.
- `orders.sales_tax` (boolean, default false): the sale's box, readable like every order column. No INSERT or UPDATE grant: it changes only through `set_order_sales_tax(p_order_id, p_sales_tax, p_idempotency_key)` (an admin, or the sale's own rep, `SALES_TAX_NOT_ALLOWED`; not a cancelled or voided sale, `SALES_TAX_ORDER_CLOSED`; replay-safe), which also moves the sale's chemical-sale drafts on or off. It re-saves each draft through `save_invoice` (its own derived key) so a split payer's fingerprint stays right. Posted and unposted invoices keep the tax they were posted with. Its own `order_updated` audit entry; the recompute's `invoice_updated` entries carry the money.
- `invoice_sales_tax_rates` (one row per taxed invoice: rate, when): recorded from Settings as a chemical-sale invoice is inserted for a taxed sale, or when the box is ticked. A draft keeps its rate when Settings changes; new drafts take the new rate. A draft switched between chemical sale and misc charge follows its sale's box again (an ordinary save, which names the type, changes nothing); an invoice never changes sale (`INVOICE_SOURCE_LINEAGE_IMMUTABLE`). Authenticated users may only read it, for invoices they may read; no write grant.
- A taxed chemical-sale draft gets one line, `charge_type = 'sales_tax'`: round((every line but the two computed ones, less the standing discount) x rate / 100), to the cent, half up. Freight and other charges are taxed; every payer on a split follows the one box (Mason 2026-11-09), each on its own share.
- The line is written by the standing discount's commit-time recompute (`_recompute_standing_discount_20261231`, re-emitted, pinned to its 20261231110000 body): the discount first, then the tax, both lines and the total in one statement. So every writer, a re-save (lines sent back are replaced), a hand edit, move or delete, and the split fingerprint are covered exactly as for the discount. The discount line sits right after the last other line and the tax line after it.
- Only a chemical sale carries the line (`SALES_TAX_LINE_SCOPE`); a draft switched to another type loses both computed lines at commit. Posting refuses a stale tax line (`SALES_TAX_STALE`).
- Returns credit the tax paid: `_standing_discount_net_cents_20261231` (re-emitted, pinned) adds each billed product line's share of its invoice's tax line (spread over the taxed lines, rounded on running totals, so the shares add up exactly), and `_return_customer_line_share` (re-emitted, pinned) prices a line on a taxed invoice from what was billed. The credit memo's lines carry the tax inside their amounts (no separate tax line on the credit).
