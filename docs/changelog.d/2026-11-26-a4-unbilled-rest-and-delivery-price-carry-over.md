## 2026-11-26 — A4: a short bill stays on Unbilled; a price change never carries to the next delivery

- New migration `20270308170000_unbilled_rest_and_delivery_price_carry_over.sql` (not applied anywhere yet), Mason's two pre-trial billing fixes (Slack 25 Nov; plan `docs/research/2026-11-25-chemical-sales-final-plan.md`).
- **Delivery price carry-over.** A delivery's draft priced its units as the order line's allocation minus the cents already billed, so a price change on one delivery's invoice came back on the next: 10 at $40, the first 6 billed at $35 ($210), the last 4 drafted at $190. Each delivery now carries its own units' share of the order line ($160), whatever another invoice was changed to (up or down). Rounding across partial deliveries and voids is unchanged.
- **A4.** A delivery stayed off the Unbilled list once any invoice touched it: 10 delivered, the line lowered to 6, and 4 units were never billed. New view `delivery_billing_remainders` compares delivered and billed units per delivery and order line (a whole-order invoice covers the order's deliveries in completion order). The dashboard's "Delivered, not invoiced", the Office Cockpit tile, Integrity Cleanup and the delivery page now keep such a delivery listed with "4 of 10 not billed ($160.00)", and Create Invoice ("Bill the rest…") drafts exactly the rest: on the delivery, or on the order when a whole-order invoice covers it. A Bill Split sale is refused with `BILL_SPLIT_REST_NOT_SUPPORTED` (void its group and redraft); a field-split order stays refused as before.
