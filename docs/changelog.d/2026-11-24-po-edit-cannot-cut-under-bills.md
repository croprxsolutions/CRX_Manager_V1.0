## 2026-11-24 - KI-151 follow-up: Edit PO cannot cut a PO's total under its vendor bills

- Migration `20270302020000_po_edit_cannot_cut_under_bills.sql` (Mason, 2026-11-23, answering Taha's #2266 review): `save_purchase_order` (Edit PO) refuses a save that lowers the PO's ordered total (a lower quantity, a dropped line, or an admin's lower cost on a line not yet received) and leaves the active vendor bills more than 5% over it (`PO_EDIT_BELOW_BILLED`; no amounts in the message, amounts in DETAIL for admins only). The same ordered-total rule `set_purchase_order_item_costs` already applies to a cost cut. A save that keeps or raises the total is never refused. That check, and the amounts it shows, is admin-only. On a PO with any active vendor bill a sales rep may not remove a line, lower a quantity or change a line's product or unit (`PO_EDIT_NEEDS_ADMIN`; Mason, 2026-11-24, answering Sol's review), so whether a rep's save goes through never reveals a cost or a bill's size; a rep can still raise quantities or add lines, and can cut a PO with no bills.
- Reversed receipts: no code change. A cost cut is refused on what arrived only when the bills were within it before the cut, so a bill over what arrived because a receipt was reversed does not block a correction (Mason, 2026-11-23). The proof shows it with real receipts and reversals.
