## 2026-11-24 — Sol skips: exact replies only

**Decision (Mason, 2026-11-24, DM to Taha):** a Sol skip counts only for an exact reply: `skip` (every finding shown) or `skip 1 3` (those numbers), with an optional reason on a second line that is logged word for word.
**Why:** free-text `yes, <reason>` (2026-11-22) read `yes, skip 1` and `yes, except 2` as skipping every finding (#2271), and Sol kept finding new wordings (`yes, I object to waiving these`, `yes, subject to Taha signing off`) that a word list could not close.
**What this forbids/implies:**
- `yes, <reason>`, `I disagree`, `don't fix`, `accept`, `skip them` and the looser 2026-11-22 forms (`Skip I agree skip it`) skip nothing. One that starts with skip or yes is refused out loud.
- The reason line must pass the public-log check (one line, two words or more, no money, emails, phone numbers, `#numbers` or markup); otherwise nothing is skipped and he is asked again.
- A skip receipt recorded under the old wording no longer verifies; he replies again with `skip`. Skips already logged and carried keep their meaning.
