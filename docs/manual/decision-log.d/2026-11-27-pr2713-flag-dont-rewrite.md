## 2026-11-27 — Quote acres: a field change flags lines, never rewrites them (#2713)

**Decision (Mason, 2026-11-27, in chat: "Flag, don't rewrite", for #2713):** after Sol round 6 found two more HIGHs in the logic that dropped and refilled copied acres on a field change, Mason chose to remove that logic. Acres are auto-filled once, when a new line is added. Changing, replacing or clearing a section's field (by hand or by a customer change) never changes acres already on a line; every line with acres in that section is highlighted and included in the "Are these acres right?" question at Save and Download PDF. Lines with no acres still take a newly picked field's acres, also highlighted. Download PDF refuses a rate line with no acres, as Save already does.
**Why:** the drop-and-refill logic produced a new BLOCKER/HIGH in four Sol rounds (a cleared field could export a $0 line; a reloaded quote could keep stale copied acres silently). Flagging asks the rep instead of guessing, so no amount changes without a check.
**What this forbids/implies:** this replaces the #1049 behaviour where clearing or replacing a field dropped the acres it filled in. Picking the same field again changes nothing. Changing a line's product asks again about acres that were auto-filled or flagged.
