1. The main path

① Quote / Booking

Quotes → Quote Builder
DoBuild sections (field, products, rate, acres, price). Per section pick Needed-by, Timing (Pre-plant / In season / Late summer) and Confidence (Firm / Likely / Maybe) STAGING. Optional "Planned program" switch. Send it.
Stock
  • Draft (not Planned): counts nowhere.
  • Sent: shows on the Purchasing tab only (advice, nothing reserved).
  • Only the old Planned switch puts a real hold on shelf stock — and it ignores Firm/Likely/Maybe.
MoneySending locks the price until expiry (season end). Raising a price later needs "Grower agreed".
Gaps
  • Holds still follow "Planned", not "Firm".
  • Hold quietly ends 14 days after Needed-by.
  • Duplicate / Roll over drop fields, dates, timing, confidence.

② Purchasing

Inventory → Purchasing tab STAGING · Purchase Orders
DoRead "To buy" and "Order X by <date>" per product, then create a PO by hand.
Stock
  • Shelf + POs by arrival date are matched to needs in date order, with a running balance (not month by month).
  • To buy = Firm + orders + jobs + holds. Could add = Likely. Maybe shown, never bought.
  • Receiving a PO adds on-hand stock.
MoneyReceiving does not change product cost; cost comes from the catalog price list.
Gaps
  • No "70% of Likely" setting — Likely is all or nothing.
  • No "make a PO from this" button.
  • Old Forecast view still compares each month to the whole warehouse.

③ Order

Convert / Partial Order / New Order
DoConvert the whole booking, or take Partial Orders as the grower calls. Extra beyond the booking can be priced today's or booked price.
Stock
  • Order quantity becomes "committed" (prebooked). Shelf count unchanged.
  • Booking holds shrink unit for unit as product is drawn.
  • Shortage = warning only.
Money
  • Order line keeps the booked price and the cost from quote time.
  • Commission is created now, on planned profit.
Gaps
  • Order warnings ignore stock held for another grower's booking.
  • Editing an order line re-stamps today's cost (profit & commission shift).
  • Close-Short ignores open partial orders.

④ Delivery

New Delivery → Confirm → Complete
DoSchedule, driver confirms (starts), driver completes. Short loads leave a remainder for a follow-up.
Stock
  • Complete is the only step that takes stock off the shelf and clears the matching "committed".
  • Negative stock allowed (flagged for review).
  • Void (admin) puts it back — blocked once invoice is posted.
MoneyCompleting makes a draft invoice automatically, at the order-line price.
Gaps
  • An order line can be cut below what's on an open delivery.
  • Returns restock but never reduce commission.

⑤ Invoice

Invoices → Invoice Detail
DoReview the draft, add freight/misc lines, Post.
StockNone. Can't bill more than was delivered (database cap).
Money
  • Posting locks amounts, starts the due date (Net 30) and AR.
  • Closed months refuse changes.
  • Sales tax + standing discount lines STAGING, switched off.
Gaps
  • Void + re-bill leaves the rep's commission cancelled for good.
  • Draft price can be edited; nothing re-checks it against the sent quote.

⑥ Payment & Commission

Payments · Prepay · Commission Payments · Month-End
DoRecord payment against posted invoices; apply prepay; pay reps; close the month.
StockNone.
Money
  • Payment lowers invoice balance; overpay becomes a prepay credit.
  • Prepay can pay any posted invoice of that customer.
Gaps
  • Commission can be paid before the sale is invoiced or paid.
  • Prepay is not tied to a booking and doesn't lock price.
  • Live Month-End "payments received" reads an old table (fix is on staging).

2. Your "planned vs firm" idea — where each kind of quote counts

Draft quote

Counts nothing.
(Exception: a draft with "Planned" on still holds stock.) LIVE

Sent · Maybe

Shown on Purchasing.
Never bought for, never held. STAGING

Sent · Likely

"Could add" column — the full amount, no % factor. Not held. STAGING

Sent · Firm

"To buy" column, with "Order X by date". Not yet held on the shelf (still needs Planned switch). STAGING

Order

"Committed" — real reservation. Counts in To buy. LIVE

Delivered

Leaves the shelf. Invoice drafted. LIVE
Your 70% idea: NOT BUILT Today Purchasing shows "To buy" (Firm) and "Could add" (all of Likely). Adding a setting like "buy for 70% of Likely" would be a small, display-only change on the Purchasing tab — it never touches stock or money. It also fits your rule that quotes don't count until you're confident: Maybe = 0%, Likely = your %, Firm = 100%, Order = 100% and reserved.

3. Side doors (other ways stock and money move)

Quick Delivery

Skips the quote: makes order + delivery + draft invoice at once, at the customer's tier price and today's cost. Stock comes off at Complete.

New Order (direct)

No booking. Commits stock, uses today's cost. Same delivery → invoice path.

Returns

Unopened product goes back on the shelf; a credit memo reverses the sale. Commission is not reduced.

Purchase Orders

Create → submit → receive (adds stock). Over-receiving needs an admin. Cost is not updated from the vendor price.
