# Independent plan review

The plan has **72 findings**. The most serious are that Phase B’s section draws do not yet preserve the price tier and reversal history that current orders rely on; Phase C’s “one draft invoice per job” conflicts with the existing multi-owner invoice path; and Prepay cannot safely ship independently of Phase B’s expiry changes. These are plan findings from the supplied packet, not results from running the app.

The owner’s fixed decisions remain the target: Firm drives buying suggestions, holds do not release automatically, competing orders warn but proceed, and applicator completion creates an invoice.
