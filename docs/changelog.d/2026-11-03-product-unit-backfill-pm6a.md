## 2026-11-03 — WP-PM-6a: product "Unit" back-fill (candidate, not applied)

New data migration `20261104200000_product_unit_backfill.sql`. It sets `products.unit_size` to the product's `inventory_unit` on the nine rows Mason approved one by one on 2026-11-03: one spelling fix (`LB` to `Lb`) and eight blank Units. The rows are named by id only. Why it matters: job and crop-program price math reads `unit_size` as the billed stock unit, so a blank Unit could leave a job line unpriced or unconverted. Old order, quote, job, invoice, delivery and PO lines keep their own stored units and amounts, and the file refuses if any other column of the nine rows moves (prices, per-acre prices, pricing version). A new trigger, `trg_keep_product_unit`, stops a product page opened before the fix from writing the old Unit back. Once a product's Unit matches its inventory unit, every later update must keep them equal. It overwrites existing rows, so the live apply waits for Mason's `I approve` on this file. Record and proof: `docs/reference/migrations.d/20261104200000_product_unit_backfill.md`, `scripts/smoke/prove-product-unit-backfill-real-schema.mjs`.

**Not in this change.** New products can still be saved with a blank Unit: the new-product form starts it as `''`. That is WP-PM-6b's new-product flow. The new trigger is only a first piece of the build sheet's "one canonical priced unit enforced in PostgreSQL": it keeps a matching Unit matched, but does not yet require new products to match. The rest remains to settle.
