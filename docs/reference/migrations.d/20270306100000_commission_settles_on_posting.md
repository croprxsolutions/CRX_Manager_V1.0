**LOCAL CANDIDATE — NOT APPLIED.** M4: commission settles on the profit after the standing discount when a chemical-sale invoice is posted, voided or unposted; never on sales tax (Mason, 2026-11-09: "commission on the profit after discounts"). `20270306100000_commission_settles_on_posting.sql` (SQL sha256: `8933ada97f87fca3b53c8393fc251ed925f13458a54d59614ce1642c368b6c9e`).

**What it does.**
- `_order_commission_base_20270306(sale)` = `total_profit + D × booked ÷ billed`: D the standing-discount cents (never tax) on the sale's lines of posted, overdue or paid chemical sales (`_order_commission_lines_20270306`: the line's order line is the sale's, or it has none and the header is the sale); billed exactly as the P&L counts it (posted chemical-sale and misc-charge invoices whose header is the sale, no application fees); booked `orders.total_price` (unusable when not finite, as the P&L reads it). The sale's counted lines on legacy invoices with a NULL header `order_id` are billed too (their discount is in D; the P&L counts them as nothing, so it earns less there, never more), so the rows sit on the rule's base. Fully billed: `total_profit + D`. Nothing posted: `total_profit`. The P&L earns commission by the share billed over the same invoices, so it earns exactly the posted profit after its discount (Taha, rule "b").
- `_settle_order_commission_20270306(sale)`: when something must move, takes the sale lock (`orders` row, `FOR NO KEY UPDATE`, waited for as `zz_cap_order_invoice_header` waits for it from the same invoice update), reads again, then moves only pending, unbatched rows whose stored base differs: each its own percentage of the base, exact by largest remainder for the whole live set, otherwise its own rounded share. Locks only rows it moves, re-reads them, writes only those. A sale with no posted discount that never carried a standing-discount line is untouched (legacy rows; every sale on live while the M4 switch is off); one that did has its rows put back on the plain profit.
- `invoices_settle_commission_20270306` and `invoices_delete_settle_commission_20270306`: plain row triggers on invoices entering or leaving the posted chemical-sale set (status, deleted_at, invoice_type), moving to another sale while in it, or deleted while in it (post, group post, unpost, void, Fix Delivery's void, deleted-delivery recovery post). `invoice_items_settle_commission_20270306`: a statement trigger on lines inserted into an invoice that already counts, settling each sale they touch once. None can be today (an invoice can't be inserted posted, `trg_invoice_draft_insert`; a posted invoice refuses new lines, `trg_refuse_posted_invoice_child_write`); it keeps commission right if M6 (edit a posted invoice) lifts that lock for inserts.
- Re-emitted with one call each, bodies pinned by md5 in the preflight: `_insert_commissions_for_order` (7 sale creators), `fix_delivery` (restored rows), `cancel_order` (Cancel Remaining), `void_commission_payment` (locks the payout's sales first in id order, then settles them). `update_order_items` isn't: a sale can only be edited before any delivery.
- Sales with a posted discounted invoice are settled once at apply (live: none while the M4 switch is off).

**Not changed:** field-job commission, reports, the M4 switches. Drafts no longer move commission at all.

**Known limits:** paid or batched commission keeps its amount when a later posted invoice brings more discount (returns KI-13; payout eligibility, Sol r6 H6: follow-up). Lines with different margins are prorated by revenue, as the P&L does (Sol r6 H5, accepted by Taha). A direct SQL change to `total_profit`, `total_price` or a commission row outside these app paths waits for the sale's next posting. An in-place edit or relink of a line on an invoice that already counts is refused today (`INVOICE_POSTED_LOCKED`, cost columns aside); when M6 (edit a posted invoice) allows it, M6 must settle the sales it touches (Sol posting r5 HIGH 5, accepted). Returns and credit memos don't take commission back (Sol posting r5 HIGH 2, accepted: KI-13).
