## 2026-11-25 - PAR-1: EPA correction waits until the product's work in progress is finished (#2579)

- Correcting a product parent's EPA number is refused while any of its sizes has a scheduled, in-progress, or completed-but-not-invoiced job, a blend ticket still unbilled or without its application record (unless its live job lists the same product: then the job counts it), or a draft or unposted invoice (`PRODUCT_PARENT_EPA_OPEN_WORK`, on preview and save).
