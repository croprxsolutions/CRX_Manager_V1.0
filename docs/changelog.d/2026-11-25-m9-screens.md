## 2026-11-25 - M9 screens: refunds paid outside CRX, returned checks, prepay balance check

Screens for the M9 database work (#2416), built to the approved design (25 Nov, v73). Admin only, like the database functions.

- Customer > Financials:
  - The Prepay Credits card has a "Record refund or returned check" button.
  - It shows "Doesn't tie" when the stored prepay is not what the credits add up to, with both numbers on screen.
  - A new Prepay History card lists every change: received, applied, refunded outside CRX, and taken back by a returned check, each with the balance after it.
- One dialog, two choices:
  - **Refund paid outside CRX:** amount, date paid, how it was paid, reference and reason. It takes the oldest prepay first or a credit you pick, and confirms with the balance change. A second refund with the same reference asks first.
  - **Check came back (bounced):** pick a check from the last 12 months. Prepayment Manager checks are listed too; a check split into buckets is one row. Returned and edited or deleted checks are shown but can't be picked.
  - It shows what happens: invoices back to unpaid (Overdue when past due), the prepay that comes off, and the check total. It takes two dates: the day the bank returned it, and the entry date, which is always today.
- "Mark check as returned" opens the same form with the check already picked:
  - from a check payment line's ⋯ menu on the invoice (the invoice now lists its payments);
  - from Payment History's ⋯ menu. Void payment moved into that menu as its red last item. A returned check shows "Returned" and offers no Void.
- Month-End:
  - The new "Prepay balances tie" checklist row lists each customer that does not tie (stored, credits add up to, difference) with Open history. The close stays blocked until they tie.
  - The Period Summary shows "Prepay refunded" and "Checks returned".
- Prepayment Manager: a notes-only edit of a locked credit no longer sends a balance (Sol r14 MED 19).
- Plain messages for the M9 refusals (for example a customer whose prepay records don't add up must be handled by hand).
- Not in this change: the "Repair…" button. No database function repairs one customer's balance with a logged reason yet.
