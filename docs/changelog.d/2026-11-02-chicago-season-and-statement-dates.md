## 2026-11-02 — Season, customer statement and CSV dates follow Chicago time (local candidate)

Migration `20261102230000_chicago_season_and_statement_dates.sql` changes two read-only functions. `current_season()` read the database's UTC date, so the season rolled to the next year at about 7pm Central on September 30; it now reads the America/Chicago date, and every caller (season column defaults, prepay check splits, field dashboard, customer prep card, call lists and the rest) follows without change. Not changed: `invoices.invoice_date` and the split-invoice path still default to the UTC `CURRENT_DATE`, so in the 30 Sept 7 pm–midnight Central window a defaulted invoice can carry a 1 Oct date with the 30 Sept season (KNOWN_ISSUES updated). The customer statement (`_get_customer_statement_scoped_impl`, behind `get_customer_statement`) dated prepay applications and undated allocation-set payments by their UTC date while write-offs already used Chicago; both now use the Chicago date, so an evening entry stays on its own day and month. The statement's unused default date arguments still read the UTC date; the app always passes both dates. Nothing else in either function changes: a preflight refuses to run unless both live bodies, the statement's grants and settings match what was reviewed, and reverting the two statement lines reproduces the live body digest exactly.

Screens: CSV exports no longer show a date-only value (delivery date, invoice date and so on) one day early in US time zones (`fmtDateCSV` now reads date-only values as local calendar days; the separate `fmtDateOnlyCSV` is removed and its Reports callers use `fmtDateCSV`). To Ship shows delivery and arrival dates on the right day and stops marking today's deliveries overdue after 7pm (`daysSinceBusinessDate` counts calendar days to today's Chicago date on any device clock, tested at 7:30pm Central and on the fall-back night). The Field Dashboard shows application dates as stored. The customer page's 90-day activity window starts on the local day.

Proof: `scripts/smoke/prove-chicago-season-and-statement.mjs` (disposable container from the schema baseline) shows the old season reads 2027 at 7:30pm Central on Sept 30 and the new one reads 2026 until Central midnight, in UTC and Tokyo sessions; a prepay application at 7:30pm and a payment at 11:59pm Central on Oct 31 appear on the October statement (the old function moved them to November); the exact migration applies, refuses a drifted body and keeps grants. A live read-only check on 2026-11-02 found no prepay applications and no undated payment allocation sets, so no existing statement changes. This candidate has not been applied to production.
