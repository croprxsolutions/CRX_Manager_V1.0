## 2026-11-23 - RT-4a: a button to switch a product group to its approved rate, and back

- Product page, Parent panel: a "Rate in use" row (Old rate / Approved rate) and an admin-only button, "Switch to approved rate" or "Switch back to old rate" (Mason, 2026-11-23). Contract: `docs/plans/2026-11-23-product-RT4a-cutover-button-CONTRACT.md`.
- The button previews first (`preview_parent_rate_cutover`): per size, the old and new rate and unit, suggested rate, and tier 1–3 per-acre prices, plus the open quotes, orders and jobs that use the sizes. A refused group shows the database's reason and cannot be switched.
- Switching needs a reason and sends the preview back unchanged with an idempotency key (`commit_parent_rate_cutover`); the database refuses if anything changed since the preview. A lost reply retried sends the same key and gets the first result.
- Database (migration `20270227100000_rate_cutover_refuses_unrestorable`, ships with the button and must be applied before it reaches production; Sol review, Mason chose the database fix 2026-11-23): a switch to the approved rate is refused when the group's old values could not be put back by a switch back (it would be stuck on its approved rate, and the rate lock could not be turned off), or when field-app invoices could not convert the approved unit to a size's sold unit. The screen shows the database's reason.
- Database, same migration (Sol review; Mason, "Fix in this PR"): Add a size, Move a size and rate corrections are refused, for every group, when they would leave a product whose default rate unit field-app invoices cannot convert to the unit it is sold in (e.g. a group rated in Dry oz gaining a size sold in g). Products already like that are left as they are.
