## 2026-11-22 - KI-188 (#2179): cancelled or voided follow-up deliveries put their short items back on the Delivery Remainders queue

- Migration `20270213110000_followup_remainders_reopen.sql`: cancelling a follow-up delivery puts the remainder rows scheduled on it back to `pending` (same quantity, link cleared) so dispatch can schedule them again; voiding a completed delivery does the same for the rows it fulfilled. When the order or the original delivery is cancelled, voided or deleted the rows close (`cancelled`) instead. Before, they stayed `scheduled`/`fulfilled` and silently dropped off the queue.
- New safety stop (Mason, 2026-11-22: undo newest first): cancelling or voiding a delivery is refused (`FOLLOWUP_STILL_OPEN`, plain-English message in `src/lib/errorSanitizer.ts`) while any later delivery in its follow-up chain is still live; staff cancel or void the newest trip first. Before, voiding first deleted the rows that later delivery carried and the units were lost when it was reversed.
- Unchanged: who may cancel or void, stock and order-line give-backs, replay, invoices, all other remainder rows. Rows already stuck before this change are not rewritten (the apply prints `LEGACY_STUCK_REMAINDERS: n`).
- Proof: `node scripts/smoke/prove-followup-remainders-real-schema.mjs` (`FOLLOWUP_REMAINDERS_PROOF_PASS`, local Docker): the bug reproduces on the old bodies; fourteen cases through the public RPCs, including a live delivery two links down the chain; cancel and void replays; reverse refuses autocommit and restores both bodies byte for byte.
- Not verified: the staging database (applied by the staging DB job after merge).
