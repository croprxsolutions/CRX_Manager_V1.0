## 2026-11-03 — Lock rates (WP-PM-11a-1, local candidate)

Local candidate migration `20261105200000_product_rate_lock.sql`, not applied. Adds a "Lock rates" switch on the Products page: any admin can lock or unlock product rates (rate per acre, rate unit, suggested rate) with a reason, and everyone sees who switched it, when and why. While locked, the database refuses every change to those three fields from any screen, spreadsheet or import; an admin can still correct one product at a time from its product page, with a reason, and the correction is logged with the per-acre prices before and after. Rates are now read-only in the price workbook. The lock starts off, so nothing changes until an admin turns it on. A new admin-only rate writer, `set_product_rates`, refuses rows changed since export by product name; it is the writer the CSV import (WP-PM-10) will use. Proof: `scripts/smoke/prove-product-rate-lock-real-schema.mjs`.
