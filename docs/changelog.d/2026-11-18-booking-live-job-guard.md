## 2026-11-18 — A booking with a live spray job keeps its rules on every path (#1959)

- New migration `20270125183000_booking_live_job_guard.sql` (not applied live; record in `docs/reference/migrations.d/`).
- While a spray job that is not cancelled or deleted comes from a booking, the database itself now refuses marking the booking accepted, declined, expired or cancelled, and refuses a customer change, with the same plain message the booking save gives (`BOOKING_HAS_JOB`). Before, only the booking save enforced it: the Decline and Cancel buttons, Convert to Order and the nightly expiry could skip it.
- One exception (Mason, 2026-11-18): a Partial Order that draws the last booked product still closes the booking as accepted, even with a live job.
- Closing a booking short (which frees its held stock) is now refused while a scheduled or in-progress job comes from any of its sections, not only from the booking itself.
- A booking section that a live spray job comes from cannot be moved to another booking (not even by deleting and re-creating it), and a live job's link to its booking is fixed: to move it, cancel it and schedule a new one (Mason, 2026-11-18). A job made from a booking section must also name that booking (`JOB_NEEDS_BOOKING`), as the scheduler always does.
- The other direction too: a job cannot become live (new, re-linked, un-cancelled or un-deleted, or a live job first given a link) on a booking that is already accepted, declined, expired, cancelled or closed (`BOOKING_CLOSED`), so a job created while a decline is in flight cannot slip past.
- The nightly expiry skips a past-date booking that has a live spray job and sends its rep one "Booking past its date" alert per expiry date instead, so one such booking cannot stop the whole night's expiries.
- Proof: container prover (before: a rep's direct decline of such a booking goes through; after: the new chain passes, the rep's decline and cancel are refused, both race orders serialize on the booking row and refuse, a job attach racing a section move is refused, so is re-creating a section on another booking, and the existing chains that write jobs, quote statuses, convert or auto-expire end exactly as before: 38 chains, 11 end to end, the rest at their own fixture setup). Not yet verified on staging.
