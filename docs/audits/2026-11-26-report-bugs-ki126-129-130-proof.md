## KI-130: why AR is not shown as of month end

`get_ar_aging(date)` can rebuild a past balance only for customers with no later payment, prepay or write-off activity. It leaves everyone else out of the totals. Using it on Month-End would make an older month look like it had less owed than it did, and nothing on the screen would say so. The label fix ("Current AR (today)") is the honest version. A real month-end AR figure would need payment history rebuilt per date. That is a new feature and needs Mason's go-ahead.
