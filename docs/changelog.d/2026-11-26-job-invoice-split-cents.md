## 2026-11-26 - Job invoices split between owners add up to the job's line, to the cent

- Create Invoice on a job billed to several owners now rounds each chemical line once, as a single invoice would, and hands the cents to the owners by their billable acres (largest remainder; equal shares go to the earlier owners). The owners' lines always add up to the line.
- Before: 10 Gal at $10.00 split 50.01% / 50.00% billed $100.01 for a $100.00 line, and 10 Gal at $12.34 split in thirds billed $123.39 for $123.40 (each owner was rounded on its own since the 2026-11-22 redesign).
- Never $0 (Mason, 2026-11-22): an owner with a quantity bills at least 1 cent when the line has enough cents (3 cents across three owners bill 1 + 1 + 1, where the old split billed 4 cents). A line with fewer cents than such owners is refused by name. Some of those lines used to be billed (0.002 Gal at $10 split exactly in thirds billed 3 cents for a 2-cent line); they are now refused.
- Quantities, prices, costs, the 50-cent checks, the per-acre fee, who is the main payer and one-owner invoices are unchanged. An owner's commission base is its amount less its own booked cost, so it follows the owner's amount; the owners' bases together can differ from a single invoice's by the owners' cost rounding.
- Known limits (Taha, 2026-11-27, option B): each owner's booked cost is still its own cost x quantity, so the owners' costs can differ from the whole line's by under a cent each (tying them needs a stored booked cost, a schema change). On a line priced at or near cost an owner can bill about a cent under its own booked cost (1 Gal at $200.00 price and $200.00 cost in thirds: A bills $66.67 against $66.68).
