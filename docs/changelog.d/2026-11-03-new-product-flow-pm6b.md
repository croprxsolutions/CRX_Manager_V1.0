## 2026-11-03 — New-product flow, basic Duplicate and missing-price refusals (WP-PM-6b, local candidate)

- **Create opens the product.** Creating a product now lands on its page, with a "Now set its prices" note in Pricing while it has none, instead of returning to the list.
- **Basic Duplicate.** Admins get a Duplicate button on a saved product. It opens a New Product form named "<name> (copy)" with only category, vendor, manufacturer, product form and inventory unit filled in. SKU, size, EPA and label details, rates, use timings, notes, brands, cost and prices are not copied; nothing saves until Create. (After PAR-1 this becomes "Add a size" or "New product".)
- **Missing prices are refused by name.** Local candidate migration `20261105140000_sale_lines_refuse_missing_price.sql` makes quote lines, order lines and tier-priced invoice lines (the field-app path) refuse a product with no price for the line's tier, the way job lines already did. A stated $0 price is a price and is never refused. Blend tickets already refused a missing price. Before this, such a line was stopped only by the below-cost check (which never named the missing price) and could still be saved with an admin's below-cost approval.
- **Customer sales sheet** names up to three products with no price for that customer (and how many more) instead of only counting them; a missing price still prints blank and a real $0 prints `$0.00`.

Parked: the product "Unit" field and the stock unit on create were parked for WP-PM-6a's back-fill (live since 2026-11-03) and are a follow-up. Blend tickets still treat a real $0 price as missing (a billing-behaviour change for Mason to decide). Early on-screen checks in Quote Builder, New Order and the field-app entry before saving are a follow-up; today each screen shows the database's named refusal when you save.
