## 2026-11-25 - Booking expiry counts a mid-run draw; posting waits for a deactivation (#2097, #2089)

- Migration `20270306120000_booking_expiry_draw_recheck_and_posting_customer_lock.sql` (local candidate, not applied).
- #2097: `auto_expire_quotes` re-checks `quote_product_draws` under the booking row lock, as it already does for live spray jobs. Observed on today's code (real-schema prover): a draw committed after the run's snapshot made the run try to expire the part-drawn booking; the draw guard (`BOOKING_PARTIALLY_DRAWN`) refused it and the whole night's run failed, so no quote expired and no alert went out that night. Holds were not released (the failed run rolls back). After: the run completes, the booking stays open with its hold, other quotes expire.
- #2089: new AFTER INSERT OR UPDATE OF status, deleted_at, customer_id trigger `zz_inactive_customer_posting_20270306` on `invoices`, the #2000 new-work rule for invoices: it checks an invoice that is created (always a draft), becomes open (KI-148's meaning: draft, unposted, posted or overdue, not deleted) from closed, moves to another customer while open, or is posted (draft or unposted to posted, paid or overdue). It locks the customer FOR SHARE, the lock the #2000 new-work guard takes, and refuses an inactive customer with `CUSTOMER_INACTIVE_POSTING` (mapped in `src/lib/errorSanitizer.ts`). Proven both arrival orders on separate connections: the second one waits on the lock, then is refused; before, neither waited and the customer ended inactive with a posted invoice.
- The deactivation check (`guard_customer_deactivation_open_work_20270128`, re-emitted from its 20270129210000 body) refuses outside READ COMMITTED (`CUSTOMER_DEACTIVATION_NEEDS_READ_COMMITTED`): after waiting for a posting it must count from a fresh snapshot (Sol advisory round 1 HIGH).
- Behaviour change: for an inactive customer, creating an invoice (credit memos included), posting an existing draft, reopening a paid invoice (a returned check or voided payment) and restoring a deleted one are refused until the customer is reactivated (the #2000 rule).
