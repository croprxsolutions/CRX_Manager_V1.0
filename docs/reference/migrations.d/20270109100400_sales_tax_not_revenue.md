Sales tax on sales invoices stops counting as revenue in the two profit reports, and the two billing setters get `DEFAULT NULL` on `p_idempotency_key`.

**Why.** A taxed chemical sale's invoice total includes its `sales_tax` line (`20270109100200_sales_tax_checkbox`). `get_monthly_summary` (Month-End Close) and the P&L report (`get_bottom_line_pnl` → `_get_bottom_line_pnl_cost_impl_20261019`) summed invoice totals as revenue, so a $100 sale with $6.25 tax showed $106.25 revenue and $6.25 too much gross margin.

**What it does.**
- `_invoice_sales_tax_cents_20270109(invoice)` (internal; service_role only): the tax inside an invoice's total, in whole cents. A chemical sale: its `sales_tax` line. A posted return credit memo: the tax it refunds (inside its line amounts), from `credit_memo_sales_tax`.
- `_invoice_line_tax_shares_20270109(invoice)` (internal): each line's billed cents with its tax share, and the whole-cent tax share alone, by the same discount-then-tax running-total rounding as `_standing_discount_net_cents_20261231`; an invoice's shares add up exactly to its tax line (checked on all 955 local chemical-sale invoices).
- `credit_memo_sales_tax` (new table, RLS; authenticated may only read rows for invoices they can read; service_role read-only (DML revoked and checked); written only by `_freeze_credit_sales_tax_20270109`): one row per posted credit and credited line pool (an order line, or a source invoice's lines with no order line: charges or hand-billed products), whole cents, **frozen when the credit posts** and never recomputed. Per pool and customer, what was billed (lots posted at or before the credit) less what frozen credits took; a credit takes round(its cents × pool tax / pool cents), or exactly the remaining pool tax when it takes the remaining pool cents, the way return pricing works. A deferred trigger freezes at commit; leaving posted clears the credit's rows at once (their cents and tax return to the pool), and a re-post freezes afresh. Existing posted credits are frozen once by this migration, in posting order. Advisory lock per customer while freezing or clearing.
- `get_monthly_summary`: `invoices.total_amount_cents` is revenue without sales tax , so Month-End Close's Recognized Invoices and Gross Margin (total − cost) leave it out. Same keys, no screen code change; on a taxed month that figure no longer equals the invoice list's sum (the difference is the tax).
- `_get_bottom_line_pnl_cost_impl_20261019`: Revenue without sales tax; Gross Profit, Net Profit and every % follow. Its `search_path` gains `pg_temp` (last), like its wrapper.
- `set_invoice_standing_discount` and `set_order_sales_tax`: `p_idempotency_key text DEFAULT NULL`. Full definitions re-emitted; postflight checks each body's md5 unchanged, the default, owner and grants. Preflight refuses if a second overload of either setter exists.

- Posting a return credit refuses a line that doesn't name the billed line it credits (`CREDIT_SALES_TAX_UNLINKED_LINE`); the only writer, `_issue_return_credit_impl`, always sets it, so today nothing changes.

**Not changed:** a credit memo typed by hand (not from a return): its full amount reduces revenue, as today, since nothing says what it refunds (asked Mason on #1868 whether office credits include tax). Also invoice totals, balances, posting, statements, AR, customer purchase summaries, `close_accounting_period`'s `total_invoiced_cents`, commission. Untaxed invoices give the same numbers.
