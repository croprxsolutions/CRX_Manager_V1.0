## 2026-11-02 - Customer Balance report no longer goes blank when one customer can't be rebuilt (KI-95, report gaps)

Migration `20261104120000_balance_reports_flag_unrebuildable_customers` closes KI-95's two remaining report gaps. `get_customer_balance_listing` (Reports → Financial → Customer Balance) now does what AR Aging already did: when one customer's balance cannot be rebuilt as of the chosen date, it returns every other customer unchanged and lists that customer with no amounts and an `unavailable_reason`, instead of failing for everyone. Both reports also handle the rare case of an invoice whose posting history has two changes recorded at the same moment (`HISTORICAL_POSTING_RECONSTRUCTION_UNAVAILABLE`), which used to fail AR Aging for everyone. The refusal to guess a historical balance is unchanged. The Customer Balance tab lists such customers in a warning and keeps them out of the table; its CSV keeps them with blank amounts and a note. The reason wording is shared in `src/lib/historicalBalanceReasons.ts`.

Proof: `scripts/smoke/prove-balance-reports-flag-unrebuildable-real-schema.mjs` on the real schema (both reports fail first; good customers identical; flagged customers have no amounts; current-date reports identical, including a sales rep's; access unchanged; other errors still fail; two mutations caught). KI-95's payment half (`allocate_payment` future dates) stays open for the KI-19 lane.
