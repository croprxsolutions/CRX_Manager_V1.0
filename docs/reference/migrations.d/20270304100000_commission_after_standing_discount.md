**What it does.**
- `_order_commission_base_20270304(sale)` = `orders.total_profit` + `_order_standing_discount_20270109(sale)`: the sale's profit after the standing-discount lines on its chemical-sale invoices that aren't voided or deleted (drafts included; every Bill Split payer). Sales tax is never in it. While the sale is only part-invoiced, the discount so far is scaled to the whole sale (discount × booked ÷ invoiced; Taha, 2026-11-25, "b"), so the P&L, which earns commission by the share invoiced, earns exactly the invoiced profit after its discount.
- `_recompute_order_commissions_20270304(sale)`: under a per-sale advisory lock, puts the sale's pending rows that are in no live payout batch on that base, each row its own percentage of it. Together they hold the live rows' share less what paid or batched rows hold, exact to the cent (largest remainder); per-row shares only when paid rows sit on an older base. Rows the writers minted (rounded shares, the last row taking the penny) are kept, so an undiscounted sale writes no extra history. Locks only rows that move, re-reads after locking, and writes only rows it locked.
- Three plain row triggers (`invoice_items`, `invoices`, `commissions`) mark the affected sales in a transaction-local setting: a product line's sale before and after (relinks), every sale a discount line's or an invoice's invoice bills, a commission row's sale before and after. Three deferred (commit-time) triggers on the same changes recompute each marked sale once, in id order.
- Sales that already carry a discount line are recomputed once at apply (staging test data; live has none while the M4 switch is off).

**Not changed:** the commission writers, field-job commission (field-application invoices can't carry a discount line), payouts, reports (they read stored amounts; Reports › Commissions "Order Profit" now shows the profit after discount), the M4 switches (`billing_m4_switch`, `billingM4Enabled()`).

**Known limit:** a commission already paid or batched keeps its amount when a later invoice of the same sale brings more discount (taking it back goes with returns, KI-13).
