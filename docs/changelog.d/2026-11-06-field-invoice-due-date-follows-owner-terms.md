## 2026-11-06 — Field-application invoices are due on their own owner's terms (KI-14)

- New migration `20261120100000_field_invoice_due_date_follows_owner_terms.sql` (applied live 2026-11-06 8:54 AM Central, ledger `20261106145427`):
  - `parse_payment_terms_days` reads "Net N" first, so "2/10 Net 30" is 30 days (was 2). Every other value parses as before.
  - New column `invoices.due_date_is_custom` (default false; readable by the app, also through `invoices_postable`): true when the due date was typed by hand.
  - New trigger `zzz_field_invoice_due_date_follows_terms` on field-application drafts and unposted invoices: blank terms are filled from the customer (blank customer: Net 30), and a due date that was not typed by hand is always the invoice date plus those terms. Posted, paid, voided and cancelled invoices are never touched while in those statuses, and an invoice posted before this change that is later unposted keeps a due date that differs from its terms; a later change to the customer's terms never moves an existing invoice.
  - The job transfer no longer stamps "today + 30" on every invoice; each owner of a split gets its own terms' date.
  - `update_field_app_invoice_billing` takes `p_terms_by_invoice` (one terms/due date per invoice) so split owners keep their own terms. A billing save from an app tab opened before this change (no per-invoice map) saves everything but terms/due date: the PO, notes and discounts save, and each invoice keeps its current terms, due date and typed-date flag; a terms or due-date change from that old tab is ignored without a warning (Mason, 2026-11-06: an old tab saves all but terms/due date).
  - Existing editable field invoices are touched once so they carry their own terms. No existing due date moves (it may already be on a document the customer holds): a date that is not its terms' date is kept and marked typed, and the screen re-calculates it in one click; a draft with no date gains its terms' date. The postflight refuses any moved date. Live: one invoice, its date unchanged.
- Proof script `scripts/smoke/prove-field-invoice-due-date-follows-terms-real-schema.mjs` (scratch container; never live). `scripts/smoke/undo-paths/harness.mjs` gains `buildContainer({ stopBefore })` so a proof can seed rows before its candidate applies.
- Field-application screen (same PR, deployed right after the live apply): one Payment Terms picker per owner of a split ("Customer's terms" by default, never a stamped Net 30), a read-only calculated due date unless "Custom date…" is picked, and the save sends each invoice's own terms. Shared terms reader `supabase/functions/_shared/paymentTerms.ts` (re-exported as `src/lib/paymentTerms.ts`) matches the database's. The invoice PDF prints invoice date + terms when no due date is stored, and Invoice Detail reads `due_date_is_custom` for field invoices. The emailed PDF (`send-email`) picks up the PDF fallback only on its next approved deploy; field invoices always carry a stored date after this change, so nothing waits on it.
