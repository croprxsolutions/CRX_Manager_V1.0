## 2026-11-03 — Fix Delivery database functions (local candidate)

Local candidate migration `20261106130000_fix_delivery_one_screen.sql` adds admin-only `preview_fix_delivery()` and `fix_delivery()`. When a completed delivery was entered wrong (amount or product), one admin action voids or cancels that delivery's own unpaid invoice and voids the delivery in one transaction: stock comes back once, the order lines reopen, prepay returns to the customer's credit, and the order's pending commission is re-created as pending (the invoice void cancels it and cancelled commissions cannot reopen). It refuses an applied payment, a split-group invoice (on the delivery or a draft on its order) or whole-order invoice, a closed month, a return, billing outside CRX, or a commission already in a payout, and it refuses if anything it showed (the delivery's number or completion date, the order it belongs to, an invoice, its status, date or amount, an order draft, an item) changed since the preview. The month-end summary and bottom-line P&L now skip cancelled commissions, so the fixed order's cancelled original is not counted next to its pending copy.
