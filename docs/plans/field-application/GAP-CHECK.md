# CRX gap check against the field application workflow (2026-11-24)

Read-only check of the master design (copied here as `SPEC.md`) against the CRX app on `origin/staging` at commit `b3e7c85e8` (2026-11-24). No code, database or pull request was changed. Evidence paths are relative to the repository root.

## 1. Summary for Mason

**CRX already does the "office" ends of the job well, but the middle of the new workflow does not exist yet.** You can create a job (directly or from a quote), pick fields, put them in spray order, print applicator and loader sheets, log what was sprayed from the office, and post, email and unpost invoices. What is missing is the heart of the new design: there is **no mixer screen and no record of a mixed batch** (CRX's existing "batch" button only groups jobs for printing). Today stock leaves the warehouse when a job is marked complete, using the *planned* amounts, not when the mixer confirms what really went in the tank, and an applicator can mark the job complete from the phone. The phone logging is thin (a blank field quietly becomes the planned acres), there is no office review step with the 5% check, and field invoices are worked out by four separate routes with their own math (the known issue KI-198 shows two of them pricing the same job differently). Seventeen things CRX does today go against the design (section 3; 18 checklist rows are marked CONFLICTS), the biggest being: billing on planned rather than sprayed acres, a flat-fee line type, a leftover cent that does not always go to the biggest share, prices that move when a draft is re-saved, reps who can only post invoices under their own name, "Unknown" printed for products with no EPA number, and a job that can be cancelled after spraying. All the tender, sprayer and tote tracking is missing, as expected, and is spring work. **Recommended next step:** write the plan-contract for the money and stock core (batch confirmation takes stock, then one billing calculation per payer), because every February item downstream depends on it.

**Plain-word glossary:** *migration* = a change to the database's structure; *RPC* = a function that runs inside the database; *Sol* = the Codex reviewer that must approve money, stock and database changes; *plan-contract* = a short written agreement about how money or stock behaves, written before code.

**One caution:** several of the newest field-billing database changes on staging are marked "LOCAL CANDIDATE — NOT APPLIED" in their ledger records (they are not live in production yet): `20270224110000` (retire per-acre pricing), `20270224130000` (Split Billing save), `20270226150000` (job Create Invoice), `20270205100000` (typed-up save), `20270228200000`. This report describes staging source code; production may still run the older versions, and nothing was run on screen.

## 3. Where CRX today conflicts with the design

1. **Stock leaves at job completion from planned amounts** (row 30), not at batch confirmation from actual amounts. KI-187 documents a blend ticket taking the same stock a second time. The job hold is released whole, not just the used part.
2. **Billing uses planned acres** on the job route (rows 44, 50; KI-185), so extra sprayed acres go unbilled and short fields over-bill.
3. **Phone completion fills blank fields with planned acres** (row 40), so a missing answer quietly becomes the plan.
4. **An applicator can complete (close) the job** from the phone, which also takes stock (row 48). The design gives closing to the office.
5. **An applicator on the job can edit anyone's work record on it**, not just their own, with no job-status check; after completion this lasts while the job stays visible to them (about 7 days) unless it is invoiced or cancelled (row 38). Office edits take no reason.
6. **Four billing routes with their own math** (row 62, KI-198).
7. **Flat-fee line type** in Split Billing (row 51), usable only when Split Billing is switched on (off by default). Separately, the older typed-up save still has a per-acre "grower share" price path until `20270224110000` (retire per-acre pricing) goes live (row 52). Misc-charge invoices typed from scratch can take "Application fee" dollar lines (`src/lib/invoiceChargeLines.ts`); no link to field jobs was found.
8. **Leftover cent** is not "biggest share on the line" in any route whose math was traced (Split Billing, job route, typed-up; the blend-ticket route was not traced) (row 53); the build sheet and DECISION_LOG still say "primary payer".
9. **Zero-dollar payer invoices** are kept by Split Billing and only kept out of email (row 51).
10. **Prices move on every typed-up save** for any line not marked as a manual price (row 56).
11. **A rep can post only invoices under their own name** (or unassigned ones they created), and the typed-up save files a rep's invoice under the rep (row 59).
12. **Label max can be set to block** non-admin saves (rows 6, 43). Warn ships on; block is opt-in. The typed-up invoice also refuses an over-rate save while the setting is still loading (`FieldApplicationInvoice.tsx` ~1785-1800). The check uses planned rates, not per field as sprayed.
13. **Loader sheets can use a different carrier rate** from the job (row 7).
14. **A field with no split falls back to the job's customer**, not the grower it was added under (row 4).
15. **Safety columns (REI/PHI) can be left off** the applicator sheet (row 12).
16. **"Unknown" printed** for products with no EPA number once a job is sprayed (row 17, KI-206).
17. **A job can be cancelled after acres were sprayed** (row 47); the cancel check looks at status only.

**Not conflicts, noted for the build:** an unused `jobs.priority` column with no screen (row 9a). Separately, a working "Add to Batch" button and `job_batches` table group jobs for processing and printing; that feature does not break the design, but its name will clash with the design's "batch" (a mixed load, row 27) and needs renaming or a clear label.
