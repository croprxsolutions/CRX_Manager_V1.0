## 2026-11-26 — A4: a short bill stays on Unbilled; a price change never carries to the next delivery

- New migration `20270310200000_unbilled_rest_and_delivery_price_carry_over.sql` (not applied anywhere yet), Mason's two pre-trial billing fixes (Slack 25 Nov; plan `docs/research/2026-11-25-chemical-sales-final-plan.md`).
- **Delivery price carry-over.** A delivery's draft priced its units as the order line's allocation minus the cents already billed, so a price change on one delivery's invoice came back on the next: 10 at $40, the first 6 billed at $35 ($210), the last 4 drafted at $190. Each delivery now carries its own units' share of the order line ($160), whatever another invoice was changed to (up or down). Partial deliveries still sum to the line total; see the known limit below for a cancel and re-bill out of order.
- **A4.** A delivery stayed off the Unbilled list once any invoice touched it: 10 delivered, the line lowered to 6, and 4 units were never billed. New view `delivery_billing_remainders` compares delivered and billed units per delivery and order line (a whole-order invoice covers the order's deliveries in completion order). The dashboard's "Delivered, not invoiced", the Office Cockpit tile, Integrity Cleanup and the delivery page now keep such a delivery listed with "4 of 10 not billed ($160.00)", and Create Invoice ("Bill the rest…") drafts exactly the rest: on the delivery, or, when a whole-order invoice covers the order, every unbilled unit on the order. A Bill Split sale is refused with `BILL_SPLIT_REST_NOT_SUPPORTED` (void its group and redraft); a field-split order stays refused as before.
- Sol round 1 (2026-11-26, advisory): billed units are now matched to the invoice line's own order line first and only editor-added lines (no order line) by product, so one line's billing never covers another line of the same product; the rest is priced after every unit already billed on the line, editor lines included (2 units for $80.01 bill $80.01, not $80.02); with a whole-order invoice, Bill the rest drafts every unbilled unit on the order (whole-order lines cover the order, not one delivery); the confirm says it bills whatever is still unbilled when confirmed; Office Cockpit reads remainders for admins only; the screens' remainder read is paged; the rollback refuses to run outside one transaction; the preflight also pins `_allocated_cumulative_cents`. Refuted: the key cannot answer for another delivery (the public wrapper binds it to the delivery and actor, `IDEMPOTENCY_ARGUMENT_MISMATCH`); the `*-check: exempt` lines are the repository hooks' markers.
- Taha's call after Sol round 3 (2026-11-26, option c, split): this PR ships A4 and the billing-by-units carry-over fix. Create Invoice also takes `p_expected_scope` (new, rest of the delivery, rest of the order) from the screen and refuses a different one (`UNBILLED_SCOPE_CHANGED`). Product lines on miscellaneous charges keep counting as billing, as the order cap counts them (a chemical-only rule would list units the cap refuses to bill again); a legacy line naming an order line but no product counts as that line's product. Delivery Detail shows no Bill the rest button on a Bill Split sale and says when the remainder could not be read. **Known limits** (closed by the follow-up PR that records each invoice's allocation, stored cents, its own design): (1) when drafts are cancelled or voided and billed again after later deliveries, a line that does not divide evenly can drift by a few cents, about one per re-bill (3 units for $100.00 billed $100.01; 7 units for $149.00 with three re-bills billed $149.03); (2) an earlier invoice line the editor added with no order line is not counted when the next delivery is priced (as on staging today). Not in this PR: returns before billing (Mason's rule pending).
- Sol round 4 follow-ups (Taha, option a): a call without a scope (a screen from before A4) now creates a first invoice only; Delivery Detail freezes the scope when its confirmation opens and sends that; Bill the rest is refused (`UNBILLED_REST_LINE_AMBIGUOUS`, "link the line to its order line first") when an invoice line with no order line names a product the order has on more than one order line (round 5). Plain-words messages for the three new refusals.
- Sol round 5 follow-ups (Taha, option i): the confirmation also sends the units and cents it showed (`p_expected_units`, `p_expected_cents`; for a whole-order invoice, the order's rest read when it opens) and the server refuses anything else before writing a row or drawing an invoice number; the confirmation closes when the page moves to another delivery and is bound to the delivery it opened on; any product on more than one order line makes an editor line with no order line ambiguous. Known limit added: every read of `delivery_billing_remainders` computes all completed deliveries (fine at today's volume; a follow-up scopes it to one order).
