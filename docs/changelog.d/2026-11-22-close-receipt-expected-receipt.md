## 2026-11-22 - #2251: closing a customer receipt acts only on the receipt the screen saw

- Migration `20270223120000_close_delivery_receipt_expected_receipt.sql`: `close_delivery_receipt` gains `p_expected_queued_at` (default NULL). When given, the call changes nothing and answers `DELIVERY_RECEIPT_CHANGED` unless the delivery's queued receipt is that one (same `created_at`), checked under the row lock in the same transaction as the close. This closes the gap behind the screen-side re-check of PR #2256: a receipt queued again between that read and the close could still be closed.
- Unchanged: the app already deployed (three named arguments, NULL) behaves exactly as before, and its saved retry keys still match; who may call it, the queue, the worker and its rows. The old three-argument function is replaced (one overload only). No data changes.
- The screens start passing the expected receipt in a follow-up PR, after this is live on staging.
- Proof: `node scripts/smoke/prove-ki25-receipt-screens-real-schema.mjs` (`KI25_RECEIPT_SCREENS_PROOF_PASS`, local Docker, baseline plus every migration): a receipt re-queued by `_enqueue_delivery_receipt` before the close, or while the close waits on its row lock, is refused and left owed; naming the new receipt closes it; a needs_check receipt keeps its generation; the same instant written in another offset matches; old keys replay; a used key with another expected receipt, a missing row and a driver are refused. Contract: `docs/plans/2026-11-22-receipt-close-binding-CONTRACT.md`.
