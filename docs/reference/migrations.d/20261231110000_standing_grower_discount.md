A standing grower discount on chemical-sale invoices: a customer's rate, taken off product lines as one computed "Standing discount" line, per payer, to the cent.

**What it does.**
- `customers.standing_discount_pct` (0 to 100, default 0) holds the customer's rate. Only an admin sets it (`STANDING_DISCOUNT_ADMIN_ONLY`, on every signed-in path), as with price tiers and credit limits.
- A chemical-sale draft gets one line, `charge_type = 'standing_discount'`: minus round(product lines x rate / 100) to the cent, half up. Charge lines such as freight are not discounted. The invoice total is the sum of its lines, as every chemical-sale writer already sets it.
- The line is recomputed when the transaction commits (a deferred constraint trigger on `invoice_items`), whichever writer touched the draft. A line sent back by the screen, or edited or deleted by hand, is put right; a plain re-save changes nothing.
- At apply, every existing chemical-sale invoice gets its rate recorded (0%, the new column's default), so no older draft or unposted invoice takes a later customer rate.
- Any other draft is left exactly as its writer made it; only a stray discount line (a draft switched away from a chemical sale) is taken back out of its total, whatever that total means for its type.
- When the discount changes a draft's total, an `invoice_updated` entry in `financial_audit_log` records the change (the writers log the total before the line goes in).
- Each invoice keeps the rate it was first drafted at (recorded when the invoice is created or first drafted; a draft moved to another customer re-copies that customer's rate), recorded in `invoice_standing_discount_rates` (`set_by` NULL: copied from the customer). A customer's later change applies to new sales only; the office changes one draft through `set_invoice_standing_discount`. Posted invoices never change, and unposting keeps the posted amounts (a split group still unposts after a payer's rate changes).
- Only a chemical sale carries the line: it is refused on any other invoice type (`STANDING_DISCOUNT_LINE_SCOPE`), and a draft switched to another type loses it at commit. Posting refuses a chemical sale whose discount line doesn't match its rate, and any other invoice carrying one (`STANDING_DISCOUNT_STALE`). With the existing guards (an invoice is always created as a draft; an order-linked invoice keeps its customer; a posted invoice's lines can't change) no path can post a chemical sale without its current discount line, even within one transaction.
- Split invoices (Bill Split and field split) take the discount just before their content fingerprint (`split_invoice_provenance`) is written, so the fingerprint covers it and the group posts.
- `invoice_standing_discount_rates` (one row per invoice: rate, who set it, when) is the office's rate for one sale; no row follows the customer, 0 removes it. The office's change also writes its own `invoice_updated` audit entry with the old and new rate. It changes only through `set_invoice_standing_discount(p_invoice_id, p_pct, p_idempotency_key)`: an active admin, a chemical-sale draft (not an unposted invoice, which keeps its posted amounts), at most two decimals, replay-safe (`check_idempotency_intent`, `save_idempotency`, `_bind_replay_receipt_20261015`). The call re-saves the draft through `save_invoice` (its own derived key), so a split payer's draft keeps its fingerprint. Authenticated users may only read a rate, for invoices they may read; no write grant. A side table, so `invoices`, its view and its column grants are untouched.
- Each payer's invoice uses its own customer's rate (or its own office rate), so a split is exact per payer.
- Returns of a discounted sale credit what was billed after the discount. `_standing_discount_net_cents_20261231` gives each billed product line its share of its invoice's discount line (in proportion to its cents, rounded on running totals, so the shares add up to the line exactly). `_return_customer_line_share` (re-emitted, pinned to its current body) counts billed cents after the discount, and prices a line billed on an invoice with a discount line from what was billed, as it already does a split payer's share. Creation, receiving and crediting all read it, so a full return credits the posted amount and partial returns add up to it. A return raised before such an invoice posts waits until it does (`RETURN_SPLIT_SHARE_NOT_BILLED`), as a split payer's does.
- A draft moved to another customer re-copies that customer's rate; an unposted invoice keeps its rate, so its posted amounts don't move. The office's rate change writes an audit entry for the rate with no money impact; the recompute's entry carries the change in total, so the trail adds up once.
- The recompute writes the line and the total in one statement, so its own line-change events find them right and stop; no session flag is involved, so nothing a caller sets can make it skip a draft.
