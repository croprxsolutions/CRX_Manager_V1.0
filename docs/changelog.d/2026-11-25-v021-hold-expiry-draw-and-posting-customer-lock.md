## 2026-11-25 - Booking expiry counts a mid-run draw; posting waits for a deactivation (#2097, #2089)

- Migration `20270307210000_booking_expiry_draw_recheck_and_posting_customer_lock.sql` (local candidate, not applied).
- #2097: `auto_expire_quotes` re-checks `quote_product_draws` under the booking row lock, as it already does for live spray jobs. Observed on today's code (real-schema prover): a draw committed after the run's snapshot made the run try to expire the part-drawn booking; the draw guard (`BOOKING_PARTIALLY_DRAWN`) refused it and the whole night's run failed, so no quote expired and no alert went out that night. Holds were not released (the failed run rolls back). After: the run completes, the booking stays open with its hold, other quotes expire.
- #2089: new AFTER INSERT OR UPDATE OF status, deleted_at, customer_id trigger `zz_inactive_customer_posting_20270306` on `invoices`. Every write that makes an invoice open work for a customer (KI-148's meaning: draft, unposted, posted or overdue, not deleted) takes the customer lock: created (always a draft), posted (draft or unposted to posted, paid or overdue), reopened (paid back to posted or overdue), restored, or moved to another customer while open. It locks the customer FOR SHARE, the lock the #2000 new-work guard takes (FOR NO KEY UPDATE for a reopened paid invoice or a credit memo, which may go on to change the prepay balance: with FOR SHARE two payment voids for one customer deadlocked, PR #2526 review), and for an inactive customer refuses a new sales invoice, posting a sales draft, a restore or a move with `CUSTOMER_INACTIVE_POSTING`; credit memos (create, post) and paid back to posted (returned check, voided payment) go through, still under the lock (mapped in `src/lib/errorSanitizer.ts`). Proven both arrival orders on separate connections: the second one waits on the lock, then is refused; before, neither waited and the customer ended inactive with a posted invoice. Also proven: posting a draft committed earlier makes a deactivation wait (an insert-only guard would not), and two payment voids for one customer during a posting both commit.
- The deactivation check (`guard_customer_deactivation_open_work_20270128`, re-emitted from its 20270129210000 body) refuses outside READ COMMITTED (`CUSTOMER_DEACTIVATION_NEEDS_READ_COMMITTED`): after waiting for a posting it must count from a fresh snapshot (Sol advisory round 1 HIGH).
- Behaviour change (Taha 2026-11-25, to confirm with Mason on the 25 Nov call): for an inactive customer, creating a sales invoice (chemical sale, field application, misc charge), posting a sales invoice's draft, restoring a deleted invoice and moving an invoice onto the customer are refused until the customer is reactivated. Credit memos and payment corrections (returned check, voided payment) are allowed.
