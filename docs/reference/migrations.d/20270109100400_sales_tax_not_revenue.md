Sales tax on sales invoices stops counting as revenue in the two profit reports, and the two billing setters get `DEFAULT NULL` on `p_idempotency_key`.

**Why.** A taxed chemical sale's invoice total includes its `sales_tax` line (`20270109100200_sales_tax_checkbox`). `get_monthly_summary` (Month-End Close) and the P&L report (`get_bottom_line_pnl` → `_get_bottom_line_pnl_cost_impl_20261019`) summed invoice totals as revenue, so a $100 sale with $6.25 tax showed $106.25 revenue and $6.25 too much gross margin.

**What it does.**
- `_invoice_sales_tax_cents_20270109(invoice)` (internal; service_role only): the tax inside an invoice's total. A chemical sale: its `sales_tax` line. A posted return credit memo: the tax it refunds (inside its line amounts), read from `credit_memo_sales_tax`.
- `credit_memo_sales_tax` (new table, RLS; authenticated may only read rows for invoices they can read; written only by `_freeze_credit_sales_tax_20270109`): a return credit's tax, worked out **once when it posts** and never recomputed, so a later invoice, return or posting can't change an earlier month. At posting, `_credit_sales_tax_unrounded_20270109` steps each credited order line through time the way return pricing works (billed lots add cents and tax; earlier credits take theirs out at the pool's rate; this credit takes its cents at what remains), rounded on the running total of that sale's frozen credits for that customer (advisory lock per sale and customer). A deferred trigger on `invoices` does it at commit; unpost, void or delete clears it, and a re-post freezes it again. Existing posted credits are frozen once by this migration, in posting order.
- `get_monthly_summary`: `invoices.total_amount_cents` is revenue without that tax, so Month-End Close's Recognized Invoices and Gross Margin (total − cost) leave it out. Same keys, no screen code change; on a taxed month that figure no longer equals the invoice list's sum (the difference is the tax).
- `_get_bottom_line_pnl_cost_impl_20261019`: Revenue without that tax; Gross Profit, Net Profit and every % follow. Its `search_path` gains `pg_temp` (last), like its wrapper.
- `set_invoice_standing_discount` and `set_order_sales_tax`: `p_idempotency_key text DEFAULT NULL`. Full definitions re-emitted; postflight checks each body's md5 unchanged, the default, owner and grants. Preflight refuses if a second overload of either setter exists.

**Not changed:** invoice totals, balances, posting, statements, AR, customer purchase summaries, `close_accounting_period`'s `total_invoiced_cents`, commission. Untaxed invoices give the same numbers.
