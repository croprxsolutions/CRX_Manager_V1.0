## 2026-11-03 — Lock rates (WP-PM-11a-1, local candidate)

Local candidate migration `20261105200000_product_rate_lock.sql`, not applied. Adds a "Lock rates" switch on the Products page: any admin can lock or unlock product rates (rate per acre, rate unit, suggested rate) with a reason, and everyone sees who switched it, when and why. While locked, the database refuses every change to those three fields from any screen, spreadsheet or import; an admin can still correct one product at a time from its product page, with a reason, and the correction is logged with the per-acre prices before and after. Rates are now read-only in the price workbook, and the database refuses a workbook upload that would change one. A new seed or each product still saves while locked, and trimming stray spaces from the suggested rate is not counted as a rate change (a spaces-only rate unit change is, since per-acre pricing reads the unit exactly). The rate writers refuse a rate unit the system does not know for the product's form, and a rate with no unit (seed and each products excepted). Per-acre prices now must be whole cents, 0 or more (live has none that are not). The correction works only while rates are locked, and shows the tier 1–3 per-acre prices before and after before it saves (`preview_product_rate_correction`). The lock starts off, so nothing changes until an admin turns it on. A new admin-only rate writer, `set_product_rates`, refuses rows changed since export by product name; it is the writer the CSV import (WP-PM-10) will use. Proof: `scripts/smoke/prove-product-rate-lock-real-schema.mjs`.
