## 2026-10-23 - Job Detail: picking a product with no price no longer keeps the old price

Frontend only, no migration. Follow-up from PR #1049.

- **Job Detail.** Picking a product on a chemical line that states no price (no price for the job customer's tier and no tier-1 price) used to leave the previous product's price on the line, so the new product could be billed at the old product's price. The line's price is now blanked, and the existing "This line cannot be saved" check refuses the save until a price is typed. A product priced at $0 still sets the line to exactly $0. Loading a recipe or crop program works the same way: a line whose product states no price (and, for a recipe, has no recipe price) arrives with a blank price instead of $0, so a recipe saved from such a line cannot slip a $0 price past the check. A customer-supplied line is never billed, so its blank price does not block the save (it is saved as $0). Un-ticking "customer supplied" on a line at $0 (or blank) re-prices it from the catalog at the customer's tier (a stated $0 included); if the product states no price, or the tier is not known, the price is blanked, so the line cannot go back to billing at $0. On a product pick, a recipe or crop program load, or un-ticking, a positive catalog price that would round to $0 when converted to a smaller unit (for example $0.50 a gallon priced per ounce) is also left blank for a price to be typed, not filled in as $0 (`autofillPriceText` in `src/lib/chemCalculator.ts`). A server-side check for this is not part of this change (follow-up).
