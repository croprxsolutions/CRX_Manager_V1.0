## 2026-11-01 - Warn before recording a check twice, and explain refused payment retries

- Recording a payment on the Payments page or an invoice now first looks for an active payment from the same customer with the same amount and the same reference, ignoring case and punctuation. A match opens a warning the user can cancel or override. A blank reference is never compared, and a failed lookup saves nothing.
- The form locks during that lookup, which has a 15-second limit (including any sign-in refresh before it) and otherwise ends as a failed check. The warning and the save use the values that were validated and looked up. Leaving the page drops a save still in its lookup, and moving to another invoice also closes that invoice's payment form and warning. The invoice form can still be closed during the save itself, as before, and Record Payment stays disabled until that save ends. On the Payments page a matching payment that would go entirely to prepay credit gets one warning covering both questions.
- When a retry with changed details is refused because an earlier attempt committed, both pages now keep a message on screen until the next attempt, saying the earlier attempt may have saved and pointing to Payment History. Other screens get a general version of that message.
- The invoice page's retry key is now one per invoice and survives reopening the form, until a save is confirmed or the page reloads. Before, Cancel and reopen after a lost reply minted a new key, so a resend with a blank reference could record a real second payment.
- Migration `20261101120000_allocate_payment_error_shows_dollars` makes the over-balance refusal print dollars instead of cents under a dollar sign. To meet the write guards it also adds the `ACTOR_MISMATCH` token to the forged-actor refusal and an `operation` filter to the replay lookup; neither changes what is accepted or refused.
- Proof: `tests/payment-entry-ui/` runs the real app in a browser on desktop and phone against an isolated fake backend, and `scripts/smoke/prove-allocate-payment-error-shows-dollars-real-schema.mjs` replays every migration into a disposable database and compares the function before and after.
- Not covered: two people saving the same check at the same moment can both pass the check, and references that differ only by leading zeros (`001234` and `1234`) are treated as different.
