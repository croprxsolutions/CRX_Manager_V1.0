WP-PM-6b missing-price refusals: one trigger function, `_sale_line_price_required()`, on `quote_items`, `order_items` and `invoice_items`, plus `quotes.tier` (save_quote writes a changed tier after the lines, so a tier change re-checks the quote's $0 lines) and `orders.quote_id` / `orders.customer_id` (changing an order's quote or customer re-checks its $0 lines). A quote line for a product priced only at another tier is checked at commit, by a deferred trigger, against the tier the quote is saved with, so a save that switches the quote's tier is judged at the new tier. It refuses, by product name, a line that would show or bill $0 because its product has no price for the line's tier (own tier price, else tier 1): `QUOTE_PRICE_MISSING`, `ORDER_PRICE_MISSING`, `INVOICE_PRICE_MISSING`. A stated $0 price, a typed price, a price-later order line, an order line that still carries the product of a line its own quote (same customer) set at $0, invoice lines not priced from the tier, and an edit that re-sends a line with the same product and price and no more units are not refused (adding $0 units is checked). A price that rounds to $0.00 counts as $0. No data, grants on existing objects, policies or RPC bodies change.

Today such a $0 line is already stopped by the below-cost check, except when an admin approves it below cost with a reason; that check never says the product had no price. This rule fires first, so the person sees the missing price by name, and an admin can no longer approve a $0 line for a product with no price (type a price, use a quote line's price override, or give the product a stated $0 price).
