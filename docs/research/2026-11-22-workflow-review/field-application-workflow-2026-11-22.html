Side doors and undo paths

Direct “Field App Invoice” (New menu → Field App Invoice): bills chemicals with no job.
Stock: never deducted. Spray record: none. Money: unposted → posted → paid.
N1 (NEW): needs Mason’s rule on what this screen is for
Blend ticket: approve → Create App Record deducts stock and writes a spray record; Create Invoice bills it. Can be linked to a job.
KI-187 / #2063 double stock / double bill with a job
Void a posted job invoice: the job goes back to completed and shows in Unbilled again. Stock is not returned (correct; it was sprayed).
Delete an unposted invoice: same, plus pending commissions removed.
Edit or delete after completion: the database still lets save_job change a completed job (only the screen blocks it), so the bill can drift from what was deducted. An admin can delete a completed job: stock stays out and the job drops off Unbilled.
Gap 6 / Gap 7 (#2196)
Cancel a job (scheduled / in progress): hold released, nothing billed. A completion has no undo; only an admin stock adjustment fixes on-hand.

Recommended order

  1. Mason decides what the direct Field App Invoice is for (N1).
  2. Bill and deduct from the sprayed acres (KI-185); this also keeps stock and the bill in step.
  3. Lock a completed job’s chemicals and acres in the database (gap 6).
  4. One shared “already billed?” check used by every billing route (gaps 4–5).
  5. Fix KI-187 before blend tickets go live.
  6. Alert on completed jobs left unbilled; warn before deleting one (gaps 7–8).
