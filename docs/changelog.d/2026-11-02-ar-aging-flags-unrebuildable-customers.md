## 2026-11-02 - AR aging no longer goes blank when one customer can't be rebuilt (KI-95, report half)

Migration `20261103150000_ar_aging_flags_unrebuildable_customers` changes `get_ar_aging`. When one customer's balance cannot be rebuilt as of the chosen date (a later void, unpost, payment, prepay or write-off), the report now returns every other customer and lists that customer with no amounts and an `unavailable_reason`, instead of failing for everyone. The refusal to guess a historical balance is unchanged. The AR Aging tab lists such customers in a warning and says its totals exclude them; the CSV keeps them with blank amounts; the Accounts Receivable strip says Total Owed excludes them and shows Net Position as not available; the customer page shows Outstanding as "Not available" (and a failed balance read as could-not-load, not $0); the context card treats a flagged row as a load failure, never $0.

Proof: `scripts/smoke/prove-ar-aging-flags-unrebuildable-real-schema.mjs` on the real schema (fails first; good customers identical; the flagged one has no amounts; current-date report identical; admin-only; mutation caught). KI-95's other half (`allocate_payment` future dates) stays open for the KI-19 lane.
