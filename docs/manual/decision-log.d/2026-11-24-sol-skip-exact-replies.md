## 2026-11-24 — Sol skips: exact replies only

**Decision (Mason, 2026-11-24, DM to Taha):** a Sol skip counts only for an exact reply: `skip` (every finding shown) or `skip 1 3` (those numbers). Mason's approval also allowed an optional reason line; Taha dropped it on 2026-11-25 (below), so the reply is one line and the log keeps its fixed sentence.
**Why:** free-text `yes, <reason>` (2026-11-22) read `yes, skip 1` and `yes, except 2` as skipping every finding (#2271), and Sol kept finding new wordings (`yes, I object to waiving these`, `yes, subject to Taha signing off`) that a word list could not close. A free-text second line reopened the same hole: Sol showed `skip` + `except finding 2` skipping both, `skip` + `1 and 3` skipping all, and a pasted finding sentence reaching the public log.
**What this forbids/implies:**
- `yes, <reason>`, `I disagree`, `don't fix`, `accept`, `skip them`, the looser 2026-11-22 forms (`Skip I agree skip it`) and any second line skip nothing. One that starts with skip or yes is refused out loud.
- The decision log records file, line and the fixed sentence "chose to leave it unfixed", never his words.
- A skip request or receipt from the old wording no longer verifies; the agent shows him a fresh request for only the findings still open and he answers again.
