## 2026-10-21 - Four small fixes found while building the record pickers (Goal 4, part 3 follow-ups)

Frontend only, no migration. Each was flagged during PR #1017's reviews as pre-existing behaviour.

- **Quote Builder.** When a new customer clears a section's field because it belongs to someone else, the quote lines drop the acres that field filled in: a rate line's total goes to zero (rate times no acres) and a units line keeps its total but loses its per-acre figures. Nothing is re-priced, so this does not wait for products or unit conversions. Acres typed by hand are kept, even when they equal the field's. A line loaded from a saved quote has no record of where its acres came from, so acres equal to the field's count as filled by it. This applies both when the customer is picked and when a saved field finishes loading after the customer changed.
- **AR Aging statement.** A customer with no transactions in the chosen range now shows "No transactions in this date range." after Load Transactions, instead of the "select a customer" prompt. A loaded statement shows only for the customer and dates it was loaded for: changing either, or going Back to Aging, returns to the prompt until it is loaded again (before, the old rows stayed up under the new period line). The statement opened from an aging row now reports a failed load to Sentry.
- **Quick Delivery.** Its customer search quotes the typed text (`ilikeOrPattern`), so a farm name with a comma no longer breaks the search; a failed search is reported to Sentry, and a reply for older search text is dropped.
- **Job Detail.** Loading a recipe or crop program, or picking a product on a chemical line, prices at the job customer's own tier. The customer list holds active customers only, so a job for an inactive customer reads that customer's tier by id. If the tier cannot be read, is still loading, or no customer is chosen yet, pricing is blocked with a message instead of falling back to tier 1.
