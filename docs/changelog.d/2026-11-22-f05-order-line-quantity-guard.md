## 2026-11-22 — F05: an order line can never be scheduled or delivered past what was ordered

- New migration `20270218100000_f05_order_line_quantity_guard.sql` (not applied anywhere yet). One rule, checked in the database after locking the order line: delivered plus what is on open (scheduled or in-progress) deliveries can never exceed what was ordered. It is checked when a delivery line is added, re-linked or raised; when a cancelled load is restored to open; when delivered goes up (completion); and when the ordered quantity is cut. Refusals name the product and the numbers; scheduling and order-cut refusals also name the other open deliveries, while the completion refusal (seen by drivers) names none and says to call the office; nothing is moved, deducted or drafted.
