## New findings

1. **HIGH — A short invoice can make a delivery look fully billed.** Staff reduce a draft for 100 gal at $40 to 60 gal and post $2,400. The invoice’s quantity guard only checks for overbilling. The office queue treats an active sale invoice as coverage, and Create Invoice refuses another, so the missing $1,600 disappears from the normal recovery path. **Fix:** Reconcile each shipped line against invoiced quantity and keep differences on the queue until billed or resolved through a recorded quantity adjustment.  
   Evidence: `src/pages/InvoiceDetail.tsx:2615`, `supabase/migrations/20261108160000_delivery_invoice_charge_lines.sql:126`, `src/lib/deliveryInvoiceCoverage.ts:87`, `supabase/migrations/20261215110000_chem_sale_bill_split_invoices.sql:1567`.

2. **HIGH — Editing prepay can revive a voided check’s credit.** A $5,000 check includes a $2,000 overpayment. Voiding zeros the credit but leaves its row. Prepayment Manager still offers Edit, and staff can put $2,000 back. The application path checks the balance without checking that the funding payment is active. **Fix:** Refuse balance edits or applications once the funding payment is voided, and make payment-funded credit follow its source.  
   Evidence: `supabase/migrations/20261204100000_ki18_batch1_chicago_today.sql:1044` and `:540`, `src/components/prepay/PrepaymentManagerPanel.tsx:839`, `supabase/migrations/20260611211058_idempotency_operation_scope_sweep.sql:1688`.

3. **HIGH — One check can be recorded in both payment screens.** A $10,000 prepay check is also entered in Record Payment against an invoice. The duplicate lookup reads only Record Payment entries, so the invoice can show $10,000 paid while $10,000 remains available as prepay. Two simultaneous Record Payment saves can also pass the screen check. The unapplied Month-End candidate would count both receipts. **Fix:** Give both screens one server-side check record with an audited override for verified distinct checks.  
   Evidence: `src/lib/paymentDuplicate.ts:45`, `supabase/migrations/20260716120112_gauntlet_money_workflows.sql:819`, `supabase/migrations/20270224120000_month_close_payments_received_recheck.sql:112`.

4. **HIGH — A bounced check from a closed month has no ordinary reversal path.** A $12,000 October receipt bounces in November after October closes. Void checks the original payment month, so the invoice can stay paid until an admin reopens October. **Fix:** Record a linked returned-payment entry in the current open month and restore the unpaid balance and any credit funded by that check.  
   Evidence: `src/pages/PaymentHistory.tsx:202`, `supabase/migrations/20261204100000_ki18_batch1_chicago_today.sql:990`.

5. **HIGH — Opened or damaged returns can go back to sellable stock.** Staff change a 20-gal return to Damaged and leave the default Restock checked. Receiving adds all 20 gal to Main Warehouse, and condition is included in the stock history note. A later sale can promise that stock. **Fix:** Require a receiving disposition and allow only verified sellable product back on the shelf.  
   Evidence: `src/pages/Returns.tsx:471` and `:1126`, `supabase/migrations/20260827041100_rebuild_return_credit_cogs_reversal.sql:592`.

6. **MED — A split payer can be billed for a quantity the return screen cannot take back.** A 2.125-gal delivery split 50/50 can bill 1.065 gal to the main payer and 1.06 to the other. The main payer can return 1.06 gal, with 0.005 stranded because the server refuses quantities finer than hundredths. A fractional unsplit delivery can face the same mismatch. **Fix:** Allow returns at the source invoice’s precision and keep each payer’s credit tied to the physical quantity received.  
   Evidence: `supabase/migrations/20261215110000_chem_sale_bill_split_invoices.sql:482` and `:2003`.

7. **HIGH — A Quick Delivery invoice can land in the wrong month or season.** Staff create a $4,000 draft on September 29 and ship October 2. The existing draft keeps its creation date. Ordinary completion uses actual completion for a newly created draft. The early date shifts terms and reporting or requires correction before posting. **Fix:** Date automatic Quick Delivery invoices from actual completion.  
   Evidence: `supabase/migrations/20260914100500_commission_dates_follow_chicago_business_day.sql:2199`, `supabase/migrations/20270203120000_inventory_edge_cases_1942.sql:624` and `:781`. The latter file is an unapplied candidate. The same existing-draft behavior appears in `supabase/migrations/20261215110000_chem_sale_bill_split_invoices.sql:1221`.

8. **HIGH — Month-End can close over a completed shipment with no invoice.** A 100-gal, $4,000 delivery finishes, then its draft is cancelled without replacement. Month-End can show all deliveries complete and zero drafts. Even the unapplied recheck does not ask whether the completed quantity was billed. Office Cockpit can still show the missing invoice. **Fix:** Check completed deliveries for unbilled units on the server at close, with explicit outside-billing and approved carry-forward exceptions.  
   Evidence: `src/pages/MonthEndClose.tsx:228`, `src/pages/OfficeCockpit.tsx:869`, `supabase/migrations/20270224120000_month_close_payments_received_recheck.sql:314`.

9. **MED — A voided order still inflates Month-End order dollars.** An order worth $20,000 is voided and replaced at $20,000. The old order retains its amount and the summary includes both for $40,000. The invoice revenue calculation is separate. **Fix:** Exclude voided orders from the order count and dollar total.  
   Evidence: `supabase/migrations/20261021030000_void_order_governed_split_draft_claims.sql:322`, `supabase/migrations/20270224120000_month_close_payments_received_recheck.sql:175` (unapplied).

10. **MED — Purchasing can bring July demand into an April buy.** A 100-gal order has 20 scheduled April 1 and 80 scheduled July 1. The unapplied purchasing redesign uses the earliest load date for the entire line. An April buying horizon can suggest all 100 gal if July stock has yet to be ordered. **Fix:** Use each load’s quantity and date, with only the unscheduled balance counted as immediate demand.  
    Evidence: `supabase/migrations/20270216110000_purchasing_plan_booking_reservations.sql:423`, `docs/reference/migrations.d/20270216110000_purchasing_plan_booking_reservations.md:1`.

11. **HIGH — A taxable Quick Delivery can depend on an admin noticing before posting.** The form has no tax choice, the order starts with tax off and no salesperson, and the latest tax function limits edits to an admin or the order’s own rep. At the configured 6.25%, $625 could go uncollected on a $10,000 taxable sale. Exposure starts when the pending feature is enabled. **Fix:** Assign a rep-entered Quick Delivery to the rep and require an authorized tax decision when needed before posting.  
    Evidence: `supabase/migrations/20260914100500_commission_dates_follow_chicago_business_day.sql:2193`, `supabase/migrations/20270109100200_sales_tax_checkbox.sql:6`, `supabase/migrations/20270117120000_billing_sol_fixes_staging.sql:335`, `docs/reference/migrations.d/20270117140000_billing_m4_db_switch.md:4`.

12. **HIGH — A credited return can strand an undelivered balance.** A 100-gal booking draw ships 60. The customer returns 10 and declines the remaining 40. Cancel Remaining refuses any received or credited return, so the 40 stays committed and decision 7’s proposed Close Short path stops. **Fix:** Allow cancellation of the undelivered remainder while preserving the delivered quantities and every return and invoice link.  
    Evidence: `supabase/migrations/20260721152604_block_partial_cancel_with_received_returns.sql:123`, `src/pages/OrderDetail.tsx:1623`.

13. **MED — Quick Delivery can save a different price from the one staff approved.** Staff confirm 100 gal at $40. A concurrent tier change makes the server write $44 and $4,400, a $400 difference. KI-190 covers the Partial Order path. **Fix:** Compare the reviewed price with the authoritative price and ask staff to reconfirm any change.  
    Evidence: `src/components/deliveries/QuickDeliveryModal.tsx:397`, `supabase/migrations/20260914100500_commission_dates_follow_chicago_business_day.sql:2213`.

14. **MED — Month-End counts completions by scheduled month.** A 200-gal load scheduled for September 30 actually ships October 3. It can hold up September close, then count as a September completion and go missing from October. The unapplied Month-End recheck keeps the existing selection. **Fix:** Report completions by actual Central business date and list overdue scheduled work separately.  
    Evidence: `supabase/migrations/20270224120000_month_close_payments_received_recheck.sql:176`, `src/pages/MonthEndClose.tsx:247`.

15. **LOW — Quick Delivery accepts a save without duplicate protection.** The current screen supplies a retry key. The server also accepts a call without one. A keyless integration retry after a lost response can turn 50 gal into two orders, two loads, two drafts and 100 gal reserved. No current screen was found to omit the key. **Fix:** Require a retry key on all Quick Delivery saves and keep the existing request comparison.  
    Evidence: `src/components/deliveries/QuickDeliveryModal.tsx:427`, `supabase/migrations/20261204100000_ki18_batch1_chicago_today.sql:5196`.

## Owner decision conflicts

Decision 5 was dropped. No conflict was identified for decisions 3 and 4 within straight chemical sales. Their stock and billing rules apply at the mixed-product boundary.

- **HIGH — 1 + 2, commission after payment and later corrections.** A $10,000 net sale earns $1,000. The grower pays half and the rep receives $500. A return reduces the final entitlement to $300. The next payout needs a negative $200. A corrected re-bill earns again as cash is reapplied. A write-off can settle an invoice without cash. **Rule:** Release commission proportionally to cleared cash or cash-backed prepay applied, and carry signed corrections into future payouts. Evidence: owner decisions lines 7–8, `supabase/migrations/20261103220000_period_check_takes_month_lock.sql:275`, `supabase/migrations/20261204100000_ki18_batch1_chicago_today.sql:1155`.
- **HIGH — 1 + 7, paid partial shipment before Cancel Remaining.** The grower pays for 60 of 100 gal and the rep receives $120. The grower declines the last 40. Current Cancel Remaining blocks paid or batched commissions, potentially forcing a valid payout batch to be voided. **Rule:** Preserve earnings on delivered, paid quantities when closing only the unearned remainder. Evidence: owner decision line 18, `supabase/migrations/20260817120000_carry_allocated_line_cents_through_lifecycle.sql:923`.
- **MED — 6 + 11, booked cost and eventual actual inventory cost.** At 100 gal, a preserved $30 booked cost differs by $400 from a future $34 inventory cost. An edit must not silently switch the basis used for commission. **Rule:** Preserve the booked snapshot and name the basis for inventory accounting and commission. Evidence: owner decisions lines 12 and 22, `docs/plans/2026-07-16-inventory-costing-plan.md:619`.
- **HIGH — 8, draft price changes and the booked promise.** Increasing 100 gal from $40 to $44 adds $400. A reason alone bypasses the grower confirmation already required for a booking price increase. **Rule:** Require a reason for every difference, grower confirmation above the agreed price, and admin approval below cost. Keep the agreed price on the line. Evidence: owner decision line 19, `supabase/migrations/20261101100000_quote_price_trail_and_expiry_grant.sql:696`.
- **HIGH — 7 + 9, booking draws with flexible prepay.** A $4,000 prepayment secures 100 gal at $40. After $1,000 goes to another invoice, a Quick Delivery draw of 50 gal reduces the booking by 50 gal and bills $2,000. Available cash remains $3,000 until applied. On a 60/40 bill split, that cash also belongs to only its payer. **Rule:** Track price promise, booked units and available cash independently. At Close Short, show the remaining promise and proposed cash disposition. Evidence: owner decisions lines 18–20 and 25, `docs/research/2026-11-17-bookings-flow-audit/README.md:115`, `supabase/migrations/20261204100000_ki18_batch1_chicago_today.sql:546`.
- **MED — 10, stock shortage versus order or load overage.** 30 gal recorded on hand against a 50-gal shipment is a stock shortage. Recording that shipment against a 40-gal order is also an overage. The pending guard can refuse order overages, and the completion body can cap the entered amount to the load plan. **Rule:** Allow completion on a stock shortage and preserve the driver’s actual quantity while routing any order or load overage to the office. Evidence: owner decision line 21, `supabase/migrations/20270203120000_inventory_edge_cases_1942.sql:515`, `supabase/migrations/20270224100000_f05_order_line_quantity_guard.sql:243`.
- **MED — 12, copying a program versus moving a promise.** A 200-gal booking with 80 drawn has 120 left. Current Rollover can carry remaining units and scaled acres. A fresh draft with rates and blank acres cannot move the 120-gal commitment. Entering 120 gal on the new draft while leaving the old booking counted can double the demand. **Rule:** Duplicate as a fresh draft and use a separate decision to carry forward or close the old commitment after orders and cash are accounted for. Evidence: owner decision line 23, `supabase/migrations/20261015200000_quotes_rep_own_name.sql:283`, `supabase/migrations/20270216110000_purchasing_plan_booking_reservations.sql:180`.

## Known-gap severity changes

- **Walkthrough #8 / bookings D1, MED → HIGH.** Closing the booking can leave a 300-gal, $12,000 draw open and reserved. The November 17 audit already rated it HIGH. **Fix:** Resolve every open draw before closure, including the credited-return and paid-commission cases. Evidence: walkthrough line 122, `supabase/migrations/20270129150000_booking_guard_rep_and_delete.sql:268`.
- **Walkthrough #9, MED → HIGH for increases.** $4 extra on 250 gal is a $1,000 overcharge without grower confirmation. **Fix:** Require confirmation for increases and record who changed the price, when and why. Evidence: walkthrough line 123, `src/pages/InvoiceDetail.tsx:2631`.
- **Walkthrough #11, MED → HIGH until F05 applies.** Cutting a 100-gal order to 60 with an 80-gal load creates a 20-gal mismatch. **Fix:** Apply F05 with a review of existing overcommitted loads and an office correction path. Evidence: walkthrough line 125, `docs/reference/migrations.d/20270224100000_f05_order_line_quantity_guard.md:1` (unapplied).
- **Walkthrough #15 / KI-129, LOW → HIGH until the receipt-count fix applies.** $100,000 of Record Payment receipts can appear as $0 and satisfy the reconciliation check without review. The tracker already rates it HIGH. The pending correction reads prepayment checks at current value, so a later $10,000-to-$7,000 edit can make a historical rerun differ from the saved receipt. **Fix:** Apply the receipt-count correction and show subsequent cash adjustments beside the saved close. Evidence: `docs/manual/KNOWN_ISSUES.md:998`, `supabase/migrations/20270224120000_month_close_payments_received_recheck.sql:112`, matching migration record line 1 (unapplied).
- **Walkthrough #12 / bookings F1, MED or HIGH → LOW for the copy itself.** Re-selecting fields on a 300-acre renewal is consistent with decision 12. An old 120-gal commitment surviving beside the renewal is a separate, higher-risk closure problem. **Fix:** Keep the fresh draft and resolve the old balance separately. Evidence: walkthrough line 126, owner decision line 23.
