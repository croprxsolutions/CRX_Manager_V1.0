## 2026-11-03 — Job invoices keep the job line's unit (local candidate)

Migration `20261104190000_job_invoice_lines_keep_job_unit.sql` fixes how "Transfer to invoice" labels chemical lines. It used the product's Unit, so a job line entered in pints for a product sold by the gallon became an invoice line labelled gallons. The invoice line now carries the job line's own unit, and its rate unit falls back to that unit before the product's Unit. A missing, blank or all-space job unit still falls back to the product's Unit, and a blank or all-space rate unit now counts as missing too (it used to leave the invoice's rate unit blank). The single-invoice and the multi-owner split paths both change. Amounts, cost, quantity, acres and commissions are unchanged. The applied-amount gallons/pounds column now converts from the job line's unit when the job line's rate unit is missing or blank.

Invoices already created keep their labels; the one live job invoice made from a job line in another unit has a blank Unit (its product's Unit was blank). The invoice and statement PDFs, the invoice screen and the field-application editor read the label as they did before; the editor prices a re-save from the rate unit, so the fix does not change re-save amounts when a rate unit is stored. Proven on a disposable real-schema database by `scripts/smoke/prove-job-invoice-lines-keep-job-unit-real-schema.mjs`. This candidate has not been applied to production.
