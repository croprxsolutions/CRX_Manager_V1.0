# Behavior contract: field billing from sprayed work

**Master copy (2026-11-24).** Self-contained; replaces every earlier draft (git history keeps them). Newest answers only. Business rules: `SPEC.md` stages 8–10 and section 9; numbers: `SCENARIOS.md` + `scenarios-calc.py`; who pays, grants, retry keys and rollout: `CONTRACT-who-pays.md`; order and gates: `BUILD-PLAN.md`.

## Facts (checked in code; re-check read-only before building)

- Four routes bill field work today with different math (KI-198): the field-app location save, the per-line Split Billing save, the job transfer (`transfer_job_to_invoice`) and the blend-ticket invoice.
- The job route bills **planned** acres (`job_fields.acres_to_treat`, KI-185) and sets `jobs.status = 'invoiced'` when it drafts.
- The per-line engine (`field_app_billing_sets`, `field_app_billing_lines`, `invoice_line_shares`, `compute_line_split_allocation`, `20260720214000`) is live and switched on (`per_line_split_billing_enabled`) but has never billed a real invoice. Its split is largest-remainder, which is **not** the chemical-sale rule; so is the staging-only `20270226150000` transfer change.
- Chemical-sale split invoices (`_bill_split_line_shares`, `20261215110000`, applied 2026-11-09): the rule in `SPEC.md` section 9; quantities in 1/10000 units, whole hundredths when the share allows, else 4 places (negatives take the 4-place branch). Chemical sales also have a per-sale tax checkbox (`20270109100200`, applied live 2026-11-21).
- `field_app_billing_lines` allows a `fuel_surcharge` line kind (`compute_fuel_surcharge_cents`, `src/lib/fuelSurcharge.ts`, a Settings value).
- Service prices: `application_services.default_rate_per_acre_cents` and `cost_per_acre_cents`; `customer_application_rates` (customer, service, season).
- A rep can post only their own invoices today; Split Billing creates zero-dollar payer invoices.
- Two invoice PDF builders: `src/lib/invoicePdf.ts` (browser print) and `supabase/functions/_shared/pdf/invoicePdf.ts` (email, through `send-email`; deploying it needs Mason's yes).
- No product sell-price history table exists; the job's line keeps its own price (`job_chemicals.price_per_unit_cents`).

## The rules

| # | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | Invoices are made **only when the job is closed** (`completed`) and every load it shares with another job is **settled**: its leftover is recorded and every job on it is Ready for review or closed (S16). The office can settle a load by hand with a reason (it records the leftover as best known), which settles it for every job on it; nothing else bypasses B1 | G2, Sol 6 |
| B2 | **Bill what went in the tank** (Mason 2026-11-24): per load and product, used = final contents + carried in − carried out − to tote (never below 0), split across the load's jobs by acres, then over the fields it sprayed by acres logged (S1, S4, S4b, S15, S17). The engine reads these from `field_job_usage` (review-checks PR) and never recomputes them. Service = each field's **sprayed acres**, counted once per work entry however many loads or products it had, only when the job's application service bills the customer (S32, S33). Each real pass bills its own service acres (M8). Never planned acres; the recipe is the plan and the 5% check only. A vendor-sourced line bills $0 | D1, Sol 4/5, M5, M8, 2026-11-24 b, h |
| B3 | **Money the chemical-sale way:** (1) each product, and service, is priced **once per job**, total exact quantity × price rounded half up once; all lines add up to that once-rounded amount; (2) that total is shared across the fields by quantity (acres for service) in the **spray order saved on the draft when it is made** (a later reorder changes cents only through a logged refresh): every field but the one with the most is floored to the cent, that one takes the rest (ties: earlier in spray order); a field with several split lines (B5) shares its part the same way, ties to the earlier spray date; (3) each field line is shared across its payers by `_bill_split_line_shares`'s rule with **the job's customer as main customer**, cents and quantity, as live. A line stores its quantity at 4 decimals (half up); cents are never recomputed from stored quantity × price. Field-only: a payer share too small for 0.0001 of a unit keeps its cents with quantity 0 instead of refusing. Built as a new field allocator that mirrors `_bill_split_line_shares`, with a test that both give the same cents and units on the same inputs (`scenarios-calc.py` `live_split` cases). Pins: 1 gal at 10.01 over two equal fields = 5.01 + 5.00; thirds of 603.75 = 201.27 / 201.24 / 201.24 | SPEC 9; SCENARIOS rules 1–4 |
| B4 | **No flat fee and no fuel charge** on job-sourced field invoices: `flat_fee` and `fuel_surcharge` lines are refused by the server | Sol 67, 2026-11-24 c |
| B5 | Each work entry uses the field split in force on its **spray date** (the Central date of its start time; a pass past midnight keeps its start date); a field sprayed across a split change gets one line per split (S7). Products use **product shares**, service **application shares**. Precedence: job office split (with reason, for every spray date) → field split on the spray date → 100% to the grower the field was added under. A legacy job share row bills as an office split; a version with `history_known = false` bills as stored and the draft says so | Q3, Sol 41, A1, GR5 |
| B6 | **Prices:** locked quote or booking prices carry forward. Otherwise, on the job's **first spray day** (Central date of the first entry with acres above zero) each unlocked product's current price is copied onto the job's line; that is the job's **one price per product**. The **service rate** is the job customer's season rate for the job's service (`customer_application_rates`, season = `jobs.season`) if set, else the service's default rate, copied the same day; one rate per job. The copy is made by the work-entries PR when the first entry with acres is saved; a late paper entry takes the price on the day it is entered, and the draft shows that date. A substitute's price is copied when the load that adds it is confirmed. A price edit (with a reason) applies to that product on the whole job. A logged **price refresh** moves unlocked, unedited prices and the service rate to today's; it never touches locked or office-edited prices. A substitute with no price drafts with that line unpriced (`invoices.pricing_pending`); the set cannot post until the office prices it (M9). A correction that moves the first spray day flags open invoices; it does not reprice them | Sol 66, M2, M9, S8, 2026-11-24 c, i |
| B7 | **A payer whose total is $0 gets no invoice.** Every payer's lines, quantities and cents are kept in `field_app_billing_line_payers` whether or not an invoice is made. Vendor-sourced lines print at $0 on a payer's invoice that has other lines (S9, S10). A payer with an unpriced line gets an invoice (`pricing_pending`) even while their total is $0. A set where every payer is $0 is still made, with no invoices: the job becomes `invoiced` and leaves the unbilled list | 2026-11-24 e |
| B8 | **No sales tax** and no tax checkbox. Each payer invoice has an **internal note** (`invoices.internal_notes`: never in either PDF, the email body or statements) and a **customer note** (`header_notes` / `footer_notes`, printed as on chemical invoices). Notes stay with that payer's invoice through saves, unpost and repost, and are copied to a replacement invoice | Sol 48; 2026-11-24 g |
| B9 | Any admin or sales rep may open, save, refresh and post a **field** payer set. Each payer invoice pays commission to that payer's customer rep, by the existing formula on that invoice's own profit (per-payer cost = its own lines' cost, as chemical splits do; a line's cost uses the same cost source chemical-sale lines use, vendor-sourced $0, shared across fields and payers by the B3 rule and stored on the line), recorded at posting; unpost and repost never leave two records; a B11 credit reverses the credited invoice's commission as chemical-sale credits do | F2, 2026-11-24 e |
| B10 | Invoice date defaults to the posting day (Central); the office may set an earlier one; the server refuses a date after the posting day; the month-close check uses the invoice date. The draft shows due date, season, terms and rep as estimates; posting fixes all of them on each invoice (S28) | GR7 |
| B11 | **Posted corrections:** while no payment, prepay, credit or write-off is on any invoice in the set and the month is open, unpost the whole set, fix, repost. Otherwise, as chemical sales do today: each payer whose dollars changed gets a credit for what remains on their original invoice (after earlier credits and write-offs) and a new invoice; in order: the payment is unapplied from the original, the credit is made, then the payment is applied to the new invoice up to its balance, any remainder staying on that customer's account as credit; a payer who drops to $0 gets only the credit (their payment stays as account credit); each correction works from each payer's current invoice (the newest in its replace chain); a payer newly owing gets a new invoice in the set; a payer whose dollars did not change is untouched. Labels: an invoice whose **printed total** changes is marked **"Corrected"** (the office chooses whether to re-send); any changed line or field detail with the same total is flagged but not labelled; money moves only when dollars change. Each post is a new revision of the set; the old posted version (lines, field names, terms, dates) stays readable from the snapshot. The whole correction is one transaction, retry-safe (S24, S25) | D9, Q10, M3, 2026-11-24 e |
| B12 | Correcting a spray record or load after drafting or posting flags every draft or posted invoice whose amounts would change or that carries that field; it never changes them (S26). Any edit to a job's work entries or loads (including work on a load it shares) marks that job's drafts stale; a stale draft is refreshed before it can post. An office correction after close adds a corrected version of that field's legal record; the original stays readable. The office correction has its own narrow permission; `JOB_INVOICED_LOCKED` stays for everyone else | Sol 47 |
| B13 | A hand-typed invoice (no job) naming the same field, `application_date` and product as a job's work (each work entry's own spray date, not the invoice header) warns (note optional), checked when either is saved; one with no field or date is not compared. Billing the same job twice is refused (S29) | B04 |
| B14 | **Billing switch-on date:** `app_settings` `field_billing_start_date`, set once by an admin with Mason's yes once the whole field app is live; the server refuses any later change, under a lock, whether or not drafts exist. A job whose first spray day is before the date (on the date counts as after) is never drafted in CRX (billed in Chem Man); the date is absolute. **Any job open at the switch-on, and any job on the `at_completion` stock path, is finished and billed in Chem Man** (Mason: no job will be mid-work at the switch). A job marked billed in Chem Man (`job_external_billings`, `CONTRACT-who-pays.md` FP-4) is refused too. The date is checked when the set is first made, so a later spray-date correction never strands a set; posting re-checks the Chem Man mark under the job lock (S31) | M5, GR11, 2026-11-24 k |
| B15 | **Application service** decides the service line: the "bills the customer" flag and outside company **copied onto the job** when the service is picked (a later change to the service does not move old jobs). "Not billed" writes no service line; products still bill under B2 (S32, S33) | 2026-11-24 f |

## Storage

- **One field per billing line:** `field_app_billing_lines` gains `field_id`; a product over two fields is two lines, grouped under one heading on screen and PDF. A line's stable key is (set, field, product or service, product source (CRX stock or vendor), split source); hand edits stay on that line. A refresh that splits or merges lines drops hand edits on those lines only; its before-and-after shows them before it saves and the refresh log lists them. A quantity edit (B03) is per load and product: it replaces that load's used amount with the measured one, shown beside the raw used amount, and the cents are rerun through the allocator. A line with no single field (converted old lines) has `field_id` blank and is never re-split from a field.
- **Draft tables** (shared grants matrix; office read; writes by RPC only):
  - `field_app_billing_line_sources` (one row per work segment; a line can have many, one per spray date): line, field, acres, spray date, work-entry and load-revision ids, source (`field_version` with version id, `job_override`, `legacy`, `grower_fallback`, `legacy_fixed`) and the full source payer list with both share sets.
  - `field_app_billing_line_payers`: line, customer, micro-percent, quantity, cents, override reason, `hand_edited`. The draft's source of truth (B7); `invoice_items` and `invoice_line_shares` are regenerated from it on every save; readers use its stored cents.
- **Stable line ids:** saves update lines in place, insert only new ones, delete only lines the office removed.
- **Posted record:** the append-only `invoice_line_share_snapshots` gains a **post revision** and, per line and payer, field name, spray date, source and version, the full eligible set, override reasons, quantity, cents and cost, plus the invoice's date, season, terms, due date, rep, customer note and "Shared with" names. Each post is a new revision; credits point at the revision they reverse.
- **Refresh log** `field_split_refresh_log`: per refreshed line, payers and shares before and after, the spray order before and after, scope, who, when, why. `refresh_split` gains its `invoice_draft` mode; nothing else re-reads a field's split for an existing draft.
- **Payer eligibility** (Q1): each line's eligible set is the payers in its snapshot; any of them may be re-weighted, including from 0% back up; a payer outside it is added to the field first, then brought in by a logged refresh.
- **Credits** reverse the named posted revision's remaining cents per line and payer, reduced under a row lock, so two corrections of a 1-cent line credit 1 cent in total. The set (`field_app_billing_sets`) is the job's one billing link; each payer invoice records the one it replaces, so the job is never billable twice and never stuck.

## Authority and timing

- Invoice creation: admin or sales rep. The server checks B1, B14 and the one-set-per-job link under a row lock on the job, so two transfers with different keys create one set. A retry with the same key returns the same set. A set is created in one transaction: all payers or none.
- Edits before posting: admin or sales rep, with a reason on price, quantity and date edits. Each override (share, quantity, price, invoice date) is a named field the server checks; prices, acres, rates and eligibility are derived on the server, never trusted from the screen.
- A save regenerates set membership (a payer who drops to $0 loses their invoice; their notes are kept on the set per payer and copied back when they return; one who gains a cent gets one). Every set action (save, refresh, post, unpost, delete) sends the set revision it read; an older one is refused ("someone else changed this; reload") (S23); a replayed successful request returns its first result.
- **Locks:** every set action, and every payment, prepay, credit, write-off or void writer that touches an invoice in a field set, locks the job row, then the set's invoice rows `FOR UPDATE` in id order, before checking anything; posting checks the invoice date through `check_period_open`, which takes the same accounting-month lock month close takes (`20261103220000`). Spray-record and load corrections take the job row lock first. So unpost racing a payment, or a backdate racing month close, has one winner and no half state. Posting is all-or-nothing (S22).
- **Posting:** a **separate post function for field sets**, so Taha's chemical post path is not rewritten; the shared single-invoice functions (`post_invoice`, `post_invoice_group`, batch posting) and the other mutation routes above gain one refusal: a field-set member changes only through the set. That touches Taha's functions: built in a window agreed with Mason and Taha, after 2026-12-15, with Taha's review.

## Old records at cutover

Unposted location-engine and job-transfer drafts (cutover inventory, taken inside the migration's own locks) are converted **cent for cent** to per-line drafts marked `legacy_fixed`: each payer invoice's lines billed 100% to that payer at their existing cents; a line keeps a field only when the old record names exactly one. `legacy_fixed` lines are never refreshed; to rebill differently the office deletes and re-creates the draft. A draft whose lines do not add up to its payer totals, or a shared-header job draft with several `invoice_shares`, is not converted: the cutover stops until the office deletes and re-creates it. Proof compares invoice numbers, payer, every line's quantity, unit, price, cents and cost, due date, season, dates and links before and after. The same migration replaces the old engines' functions with refusals. Posted old invoices stay as they are and unpost through today's path; once unposted they follow the `legacy_fixed` route. (After Taha's reset, live has none of these.)

## After posting

No new engine; the posting PR proves the paths after posting with one **ledger journey**: a multi-payer job → posted payer invoices → each payer's PDF from both builders (same content: only their lines and fields; "Shared with"; field total and your share; customer note; no internal note or cost) → balance and aging → a payment to one payer only (through the normal payment screen) → that payer's statement → a B11 correction that takes one payer to $0 (credit only, payment kept as account credit, commission reversed) → one payer's prepay applied to their own invoice only. Every recipient-facing read (invoice page, both PDFs, map attachments, the email link, the job-done email) is built from a payer-scoped view: that payer's own lines, plus each shared field line's total and the sharing payers' names; never other payers' amounts or any cost. A direct request for another payer's invoice or PDF is refused. Inventory every reader first (`InvoiceDetail.tsx`, `Invoices.tsx`, the field invoice panels, `fieldInvoiceList.ts`, `invoiceBatchPosting.ts`, `splitGroupPostChecks.ts`, both PDF builders, statements, AR aging, commission).

## Proof cases

Every money scenario in `SCENARIOS.md` (S1–S10, S20, S22–S26, S28–S33) becomes a SQL test as the real caller on a migrated database, expected cents from the tables, plus:

1. A job with an unsettled shared load: invoice creation refused with a plain message; after a hand settle with a reason: allowed.
2. A `flat_fee` or `fuel_surcharge` line on a job-sourced invoice: refused; on a hand-typed invoice: allowed as today.
3. A rep who is not the customer's rep makes, edits and posts a field set; each payer invoice's commission goes to that payer's rep, on that invoice's profit; unpost and repost leave one record.
4. A rep posting another rep's **chemical** invoice: refused as today.
5. One invoice in a set cannot post: none post.
6. Two transfers of the same job with different keys at once: one set.
7. Single-invoice post, unpost, delete, void and return on a field set member: refused. A payment and a prepay on one payer's invoice through the normal screens: accepted; the set then cannot be unposted.
8. Unpost racing a payment, and a backdated post racing month close, in both orders: one wins, no half state.
9. Credit and rebill after a timeout between steps: the retry finishes; the job is billed once; P2's payment sits on P2's new invoice (S25's exact balances).
10. Field allocator and `_bill_split_line_shares` give the same cents and units for the same line, shares and main customer (the `live_split` pins).
11. A re-save keeps every line id and every hand edit; a post from an older set revision is refused.
12. A marked job, a job first sprayed before the switch-on date, and any job while no date is set: refused.
13. `internal_notes` appears in neither PDF, the email body nor a statement; notes survive unpost, repost and replacement.
14. P2 opens P1's invoice or PDF directly: refused.
