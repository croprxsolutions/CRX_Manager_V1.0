**Shape.** One transaction with `lock_timeout` 5s. The preflight pins four bodies by md5 and refuses if any has drifted or the migration is already applied: `_complete_delivery_authorized_impl` and `update_cycle_count_item` (both from 20270126110000), `_complete_delivery_authorized_impl_20270122`, and `_guard_order_item_delivery_lineage`.

**A (deadlock on a stock row that does not exist yet).** The completion wrapper first locks the delivery's existing Main Warehouse rows in product order, as before. Then, while the delivery is in progress, it creates a missing row for each product the completion moves, in product order, exactly as the body would (0/0, `manufactured_at_delivery`, `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`), and locks it. Locking existing rows first means a writer holding one while it creates a missing row makes the completion wait, not deadlock (Sol r2). Two completions creating rows for the same products wait instead of deadlocking. A key that already has a saved completion receipt (`check_idempotency`, the body's own read) is treated as not in progress: no figure check and no row, and the body answers from the receipt as today.

**B (more than 4 decimals on hand).** `cycle_count_items.expected_qty` and `variance` become plain `numeric`; `counted_qty` stays `numeric(14,4)`. `update_cycle_count_item` stores the exact on-hand, so Complete lands on the counted figure. A non-finite on-hand is refused (`CYCLE_COUNT_ON_HAND_INVALID`) instead of stored. It keeps its `authenticated` and `service_role` execute. A count line saved before the apply keeps its 4-place baseline and can complete up to 0.00005 of a unit off, as today; Mason dropped the guard that blocked the apply for it (DECISION_LOG 2026-11-19). The preflight refuses, naming the line, if a stored baseline or variance is not a number (the new check would otherwise fail without saying which).

**C (override escape).** `_guard_order_item_delivery_lineage` loses its `app.admin_override` early return.

**D (Astra 13, stale partial figures).** While the delivery is in progress and figures are sent, the wrapper refuses before anything moves:
- `DELIVERY_QUANTITIES_INVALID` unless the figures are a JSON object of JSON numbers;
- `DELIVERY_QUANTITIES_STALE` unless their keys are exactly the delivery's line ids (exact text).

Both are `check_violation`. They reach the screens' online completion; a queued offline receipt carries no completion evidence, so it already stops earlier (`COMPLETION_EVIDENCE_REQUIRED`) and goes to office review as today.

**E (Astra 36, one product on two lines).** The body compares on-hand with the delivery's total for each product, warning and counting once per product. Two lines that are short together warn and flag review. The body is re-emitted under its current name.
