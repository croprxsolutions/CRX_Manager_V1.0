1. The main path

① Quote / Booking

Quotes → Quote Builder
DoBuild sections (field, products, rate, acres, price). Per section pick Needed-by, Timing (Pre-plant / In season / Late summer) and Confidence (Firm / Likely / Maybe) STAGING. Optional "Planned program" switch. Send it.
Stock
  • Draft (not Planned): counts nowhere.
  • Sent (not Planned): counts on the Purchasing tab only (advice, nothing reserved on the shelf).
  • Only the Planned switch puts a real hold on shelf stock — on every line, whatever its Firm/Likely/Maybe. A Planned draft also counts in Purchasing like a sent booking STAGING.
  • Holds drop once the booking is accepted/converted; drawing product shrinks them.
MoneySending saves the price. Expiry defaults to season end (staff can pick another date); Planned or part-drawn bookings don't auto-expire. Lowering a price is always allowed; raising a booked price needs "Grower agreed".
Gaps
  • Holds follow "Planned", not "Firm".
  • Hold ends 14 days after Needed-by (no date = never ends). Office Cockpit lists expiring holds.
  • Duplicate / Roll over copy acres, quantities and the Planned switch; neither closes last year's booking.

② Purchasing

Inventory → Purchasing tab STAGING · Purchase Orders
DoRead "To buy" and "Order X by <date>" per product, then create a PO by hand.
Stock
  • Shelf + open (non-draft) POs are shared out once. Needs go in priority order (shelf-only holds → negative stock → committed → Could add → Maybe), by date within each group. A late PO can cover a need (flagged) but never adds a buy.
  • To buy = the uncovered part of negative stock + orders + active jobs + manual holds + dated Firm lines, within the horizon. Undated Firm goes to a "needs a date" list.
  • Could add = the uncovered part of Likely and lines with no confidence set. Maybe shown, never bought.
  • Receiving a PO adds on-hand stock.
MoneyReceiving does not change the catalog cost (current_cost); the PO's own cost is separate and feeds vendor bills.
Gaps
  • No "make a PO from this" button.
  • Old Forecast view compares each month's hold demand to today's warehouse stock, with no running balance.

③ Order

Convert / Partial Order / New Order / Rush
DoConvert the whole booking, or take Partial Orders as the grower calls. Extra beyond the booking can be priced at today's or the booked price.
Stock
  • Order quantity becomes "committed" (prebooked). Shelf count unchanged.
  • Booking holds shrink unit for unit as booked product is drawn.
  • Shortage = warning only (and the warning ignores holds).
Money
  • Booked lines keep the booked price and quote-time cost; extra and direct-order lines take today's cost.
  • Commission is created now, on planned profit (Rush orders: when priced).
  • Editing an existing line keeps its cost; price/qty edits re-figure profit and pending commission. Booking-draw orders can't be edited.
Gaps
  • Order warnings ignore stock held for another grower's booking.
  • An edit ignores commission already in a payout batch or paid.
  • Close-Short ignores open partial orders.
  • Cancel Remaining is refused for good after a received return or any cancelled commission row.

④ Delivery

New Delivery → Confirm → Complete
DoSchedule, driver confirms (starts), driver completes (or office does start+complete in one step). Short loads leave a remainder for a follow-up.
Stock
  • Complete is the only step that takes stock off the shelf (actual quantity, Main Warehouse) and clears the matching "committed".
  • Negative stock allowed (flagged for review).
  • Void (admin, with reason) puts it back — refused while a posted/paid/overdue invoice exists (database trigger) or a return was received. Fix Delivery voids delivery + invoice together and re-creates commission.
MoneyCompleting makes a draft invoice at the order-line price on the ordinary path. Bill Split makes one draft per payer; a field/acre split drafts only when the whole order ships the same day, else it's flagged for manual split billing; Quick Delivery already made its draft.
Gaps
  • An order line can be cut below what's on an open delivery — fix built (F05) NOT APPLIED ANYWHERE.
  • Cancel Remaining also cancels a load already on the truck.

⑤ Invoice

Invoices → Invoice Detail
DoReview the draft, add freight/misc lines, Post. An unpaid posted invoice can be unposted, edited and re-posted.
StockNone. Can't bill more than was delivered (database cap). Billing less is not caught.
Money
  • Posting locks amounts and starts AR. Due date = invoice date + invoice's terms, else customer's terms, else Net 30.
  • Closed months refuse posting and payments.
  • Sales tax + standing discount STAGING, database switch off; controls hidden on live.
Gaps
  • Void + re-bill: if that was the order's last active invoice, pending commission stays cancelled (Fix Delivery doesn't have this problem).
  • Draft price can be edited with no comparison to the booked price.
  • A lowered quantity makes the delivery look fully billed.

⑥ Payment & Commission

Payments · Prepay · Commission Payments · Month-End
DoRecord payment against posted invoices; enter prepay checks; apply prepay; pay reps; close the month.
StockNone.
Money
  • Payment lowers invoice balance; overpay becomes a prepay credit.
  • Prepay can pay any posted/overdue invoice of that customer.
  • "Paid" can also come from a write-off, prepay or credit memo — no new cash.
Gaps
  • Commission can be paid before the sale is invoiced or paid.
  • Prepay is not tied to a booking and doesn't lock price.
  • Live Month-End "payments received" reads an old table (fix STAGING).
  • Same check can be entered in both payment screens.

2. Planned vs firm — where each kind of quote counts

Draft quote

Counts nothing. LIVE
Exception: a draft with "Planned" on holds stock LIVE and counts in Purchasing STAGING.

Sent · Maybe

Shown on Purchasing. Never bought for. Held only if Planned is on. STAGING

Sent · Likely (or unset)

"Could add" — the part not already covered by stock or POs; you judge how much to buy. Held only if Planned is on. STAGING

Sent · Firm

Gets first claim on stock and POs; "To buy" + "Order X by date" for what's uncovered. No date = "needs a date" list. Not held on the shelf unless Planned. STAGING

Order

"Committed" — real reservation LIVE. Counts in To buy STAGING.

Delivered

Leaves the shelf. Invoice drafted (split orders: see ④). LIVE
How you use it: Purchasing shows "To buy" (Firm + orders, uncovered part) and "Could add" (Likely and unset, uncovered part). You decide by judgment how much of Likely to buy; no percentage setting is planned (Mason, 2026-11-22).

3. Side doors (other ways stock and money move)

Quick Delivery

Skips the quote: makes order + scheduled delivery (stock committed) + optional draft invoice at once, at tier price and today's cost. The draft is dated when it's created, not when it ships. Stock comes off at Complete.

New Order (direct)

No booking. Commits stock, today's cost. Same delivery → invoice path.

Rush order

Ship now, price later: commits stock with pricing pending; can deliver; can't post the bill until priced. Commission created at pricing.

Bulk order import

Imported orders are confirmed and commit stock in a batch; shortages reported during import.

Cancel / Cancel Remaining / Void order

Releases undelivered committed stock, cancels its loads and drafts, cancels pending commission. Refused when commission is batched/paid, a return was received, or any commission row isn't pending.

Fix Delivery

One screen: voids the delivery and its invoices together, puts stock back, reopens quantities, replaces pending commission. Blocked by payments, returns and some split/closed-month cases.

Returns

Request → approve → receive. Lines ticked "restock" go back on the shelf (the tick defaults on, even if condition is changed to Damaged). The credit memo follows the billed payer and must be applied. Commission is not reduced.

Purchase Orders

Create → submit → receive (adds stock). Over-receiving needs an admin + reason. Quick Receive, receipt reversal and PO Close-Short also exist. Vendor bills create money owed. Catalog cost not updated.

Billed outside CRX

Completed delivery still moves stock; a record keeps it out of the unbilled queue and blocks a second CRX invoice.

From-scratch invoice

Charge lines only (freight, service); no product lines, no stock.

Write-off / credit memo / finance charge

Write-off lowers AR without cash (reversible). Credit memo lowers a balance. Finance charges create a new unposted invoice on overdue balances. No stock.

Stock corrections

Manual add/adjust, cycle counts, tote fill from bulk: change shelf stock outside sales, no money.
