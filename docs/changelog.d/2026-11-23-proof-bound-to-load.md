## 2026-11-23 — A customer's proof completes a delivery only for the load it was taken for

Follow-up to Sol round 7 on #2324 (findings 1 and 2) and round 11 (the delivered amounts are part of the proof: a signature taken at 0.005 cannot complete 0.05). When a screen sends the products it showed as the customer signed (`p_lines_seen` on `complete_delivery` and `start_and_complete_delivery`), or saves them with the signed-ticket photo (`delivery_photos.lines_seen`), the completion is refused (`DELIVERY_LOAD_CHANGED`, `PAPER_TICKET_LOAD_CHANGED`) if the delivery's products or the delivered amounts sent have changed since. "The same load" is the same order lines, products and amounts; re-saving an identical list does not void the proof. The check sits in the shared completion step after every existing rule, so retries, left-at-site, no-proof and offline completions behave as before. Today's app sends nothing, so nothing changes until the screen change ships; a later migration makes the binding required.
