## 2026-11-06 — KI-18: the last database dates follow the Chicago business day

- New migration `20261121090000_ki18_chicago_ticket_year_dashboard_aging_invoice_date_limit.sql`.
  - Blend-ticket numbers (`generate_ticket_number`) take the Chicago year, like quote, order and
    rebate-claim numbers already do, so a ticket made after 6 PM on Dec 31 is not numbered for the next year.
  - The Financial Dashboard's AR-aging strip (`financial_dashboard_summary`) uses the AR Aging page's day
    ranges (0-29, 30-59, 60-89, 90+ days past due) on the Chicago date, so one invoice is in the same bucket
    on both screens. The strip's labels now say 30-59 and 60-89. Its season panels and month label read the
    Chicago date too.
  - A new trigger refuses an invoice date more than 365 days after today's Chicago date (a typed year like
    2206). Existing rows are not touched; a row only meets the check when its invoice date is changed.
- The customer page's aging strip was labelled 1-30 / 31-60 / 61-90 / 90+ for amounts that are really
  30-59 / 60-89 / 90-119 / 120+ days past due; the labels now match the AR Aging page.
- Proof: `scripts/smoke/prove-ki18-db-dates-real-schema.mjs`.
