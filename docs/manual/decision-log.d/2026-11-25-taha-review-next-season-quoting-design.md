## 2026-11-25 — Next-season quoting: start from last season or a template, not a copy of last year's numbers

**Decision (Mason, reply to Taha's 2026-11-25 chemical-side review posted in #crx-work; "Yes, record them"):** for January (Taha's lane), next-season quoting replaces copy-forward roll over with this design:
1. On a new quote, start from **last season's booking for this grower** or a **saved template**. Products, rates and notes fill in at the grower's current tier price; acres are left blank.
2. Section names become a **pass dropdown**: Corn or Soybean × Pre-emerge, Post-emerge, Fungicide, In-furrow fertilizer, Side-dress, plus "Other".
3. A line at the top shows **"Last season: Corn X ac · Soybeans Y ac"**. Per crop it shows the largest acres on any one pass, not the sum. The rep types this year's acres (most growers rotate corn and beans, so the numbers are just typed across).

**Why:** copying last year's numbers carries stale acres and prices forward; reps need last year's plan as a starting point, not as this year's commitment.
**What this forbids/implies:**
- It does NOT include: a rotation auto-fill; field record changes; purchasing changes; the spray-job Crop Programs page.
- Decision 12 (2026-11-22) still holds: nothing counts in purchasing until acres are entered.
- Roll over can stay or be replaced by "start from last season".
