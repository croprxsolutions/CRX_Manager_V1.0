## 2026-10-21 — Sol carry-forward: a moved main no longer forces a full Sol re-review

Mason approved "go with carry-forward". Every merge into main used to void every waiting PR's Sol proof, because the proof is bound to one head and one base, so parallel sessions kept paying for full Sol reviews again (81 merges to main in the week to 2026-10-21).

- `scripts/write-codex-push-proof.mjs --carry-forward` (with `--dry-run` to print the decision only) keeps an earlier clean Sol review when the new head is exactly the conflict-free merge of the reviewed head and today's origin/main (checked with `git merge-tree`, so a resolved conflict or a hand edit refuses) and that review is under 24 hours old. It carries with no model call when the PR's files and main's new files do not overlap and neither side touches `supabase/`, `.claude/hooks/` or `.codex/hooks/` against non-documentation changes on the other side; otherwise it runs a focused Sol collision check (`MAIN_DELTA.patch` + `COLLISION_FOCUS.json`) and carries only on CLEAN.
- `proofValid()` in `.claude/hooks/codex-push-lib.mjs` accepts the carried shape (`codex_ran: false`, `carried_forward: true`, `carry.mode`, `carry.from_head`, `carry.from_base`) on the same exact-head, exact-base and 30-minute terms. `pr-merge-guard.mjs` prints `SOL CARRY-FORWARD` when it allows one; its and the Codex `production-action-guard.mjs` denials now point at `--carry-forward`.
- Docs: AGENTS.md, `.claude/commands/ship.md`, `/codex-review` Step 3B, `docs/reference/agent-guardrails.md`, `docs/manual/AGENT_ONBOARDING.md` and `docs/manual/DECISION_LOG.md`.
- Tests: carried-proof acceptance and rejection cases in `codex-push-lib.test.mjs`; the decision rules, source-proof rules and an end-to-end throwaway-repository run (clean carry accepted for the new base only, an edited merge commit refused, a stale source refused) in `scripts/write-codex-push-proof.test.mjs`.
