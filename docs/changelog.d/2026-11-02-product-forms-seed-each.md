## 2026-11-02 — Seed and Each product forms (local candidate)

Migration `20261104150000_product_forms_seed_each.sql` adds Seed and Each / unit. Seed uses Lb or Unit; Each uses Ea or Unit for inventory and containers. Other units are refused. Existing liquid and dry choices remain as they were. Billing keeps a matching unit's quantity, refuses conversion between different seed/each units, and leaves weight or volume equivalent blank. The price workbook cannot change a seed/each rate unit because its unit check knows only liquid/dry/both; use the product page. This candidate has not been applied to production.

Seed and Each require a stock unit. A blank product rate unit is filled from that stock unit before per-acre pricing, and setting only the form now re-prices per acre too. Every quote line's rate and price units must match the stock unit; a different unit is refused and a blank one is filled from the stock unit, so a quote made from a saved template keeps working. Product, quote, job and field-app pickers offer only that unit.

Every seed/each job-chemical line must be in the stock unit too: the database refuses another unit and fills a blank one, so job completion deducts the exact amount instead of falling back to the raw number. A product with no form cannot become seed/each while any of its quote or job lines uses another or a blank unit.
