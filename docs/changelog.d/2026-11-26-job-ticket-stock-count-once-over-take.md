## 2026-11-26 — Undoing a ticket returns stock other tickets over-took

From Sol's round-4 review of PR #2679: when undoing one blend ticket means the job's other tickets took more than the job now needs, the undo now puts that extra back itself (a flagged row on the job's books) instead of asking staff to correct it with an adjustment, which later postings would have ignored.
